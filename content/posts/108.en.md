Once upon a time, in a small and beautiful village, there lived a little boy named Parsa. Parsa loved autumn. Every year at the beginning of autumn, when the leaves of the trees turned golden, orange and red, Parsa couldn't help but be happy. He loved playing with dried leaves and walking in the cool autumn air.

One autumn morning, Parsa decided to go to the forest near the village to see what new things autumn had prepared for her. Wearing a warm hat and a colorful scarf, he ran out of the house and walked towards the forest.

When Parsa reached the forest, he was very happy to see the beautiful colors of the leaves and to hear them rustle under his feet. He began to collect the beautiful leaves to make a big bundle with them. As he was gathering leaves, he suddenly heard a rustling sound from behind a big tree. Parsa looked carefully and saw a small squirrel looking at him with shiny eyes.

Parsa said with a smile: "Hello, little squirrel! Are you enjoying autumn too?"

The squirrel replied with a nod, "Hello Parsa! Yes, autumn is my favorite season too. I'm always busy gathering hazelnuts for the winter."

Parsa said in surprise: "How interesting! Can I help you?"

The squirrel said happily: "Of course, thank you Parsa! If you help me, I will tell you an autumn story."

Parsa and Squirrel started collecting hazelnuts. Together they gathered many hazelnuts and hid them in a small hole by the tree. When they were done, the squirrel said in a soft voice, "Now it's story time!"

The squirrel began to tell a story: "Once upon a time, there lived a small green leaf at the top of a tall tree. He always wished to come down and play with the other leaves. One day, an autumn wind blew and the small leaf slowly fell from the tree and fell to the ground. He played with the other leaves and made new friends. They laughed and played together and enjoyed the beautiful autumn."

Parsa listened eagerly and enjoyed the story of the squirrel. When the story was over, Parsa said, "That was a beautiful story, Squirrel! I always liked to play with the leaves, and now I know they enjoy playing too."

The squirrel said with a smile, "Yes, Parsa! Autumn is the season where everything is beautiful and magical. I hope you always enjoy this season."

Parsa and the squirrel decided to play together in the forest every day and enjoy the beautiful autumn. Together they told more stories about autumn and learned new things every day. Parsa realized that autumn is not only the season of colors, but also the season where you can find new friendships and enjoy the beauty of nature.

From that day on, every time autumn came, Parsa eagerly went to the forest and spent time with the little squirrel. Together they collected beautiful autumn leaves, told magical stories and enjoyed every moment they spent in this beautiful season.

And so, Parsa and the little squirrel looked forward to the beginning of autumn every year to spend time together again and celebrate their friendship. Autumn was always full of beautiful memories and magical stories for them.

The End.
