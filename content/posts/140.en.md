In a faraway forest, a beautiful, adventurous life went on for the animals who lived there. In this forest, a little child named Alice lived with her family. Alice was a kind-hearted, lovable girl who always played with the animals of the forest and knew nature well.

One day, Alice went into the forest alone to pick tasty fruit. While she was gathering the fruit, a young squirrel came near. The squirrel was lively and full of energy, with soft, neat fur. Alice, with happy laughter that came from her heart, drew the squirrel close.

"Hello, dear squirrel!" Alice said.

"Hello, Alice!" the squirrel answered in a voice full of joy. "What is new?"

Alice told the squirrel that she was gathering tasty fruit to take home. The squirrel said that it was looking for fruit for winter too, and that is how the friendship between Alice and the squirrel began.

They played together every day and always laughed side by side. Alice taught the squirrel how to climb the branches of the trees, and the squirrel showed Alice how to jump from the trees quickly and beautifully.

One day, Alice ran into a problem. She was stuck and could not get up from where she was. The squirrel saw this and jumped quickly toward Alice and helped her stand up.

"Thank you, dear squirrel!" Alice said with a smile.

"It is no trouble, Alice. I love to help you!" the squirrel answered with a wide smile.

Alice and the squirrel went to the longest grasses and told everyone the interesting things they had learned. They were always together, and they never gave up on their friendship and kindness.

After that, they were the very best of friends. They always stayed together, and with their laughter they made the forest happy.

The End.
