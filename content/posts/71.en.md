Once upon a time, in a small, beautiful village, four friends named Arman, Melika, Artin, and Leila lived. In the summer, when school was closed, they were always looking for adventure and new games. This summer was no different.

One warm summer day, the children decided to go to the forest near the village and have a day full of adventure. They packed snacks and water and set off toward the forest. The forest was full of tall trees and colorful flowers, and beautiful birds sang among the branches.

When they reached the forest, Arman said, “Let’s make a treasure map and look for treasure!” Everyone agreed with this idea, and together they began to draw a treasure map. They put signs on their map, like a big tree, a strange rock, and a little river.

After they drew the treasure map, they decided to follow the signs. The first sign was a big tree beside a hill. They ran excitedly toward the hill and found the big tree. Melika said, “Now we should go toward the strange rock!”

The children followed the map and, after a little walking, reached the strange rock. This rock had a shape like a big animal, and it surprised everyone. Artin said, “How interesting! Now we should find the little river.”

Following the map, they came to a little river with clear, cool water. Leila suggested they rest beside the river for a while and enjoy the cool water. The children put their feet in the water and enjoyed how cool it felt.

After a little rest, they set off toward the last sign, a little tree with golden leaves. When they found the little tree, Melika began to dig in the ground under the tree, looking for treasure. After a few moments, she found a small, beautiful box. Everyone opened the box with excitement, and inside it was a lovely painting and a few small precious stones.

Arman said, “This painting and these stones are so pretty! I think this treasure reminds us that the most important things in life are friendship and working together.”

Everyone agreed with those words and decided to keep the painting and the stones as a memory of this adventure. They went back to the village and told their families the story of finding the treasure. Everyone was glad to hear the children’s story and was proud of them.

The summer days went on with games and new adventures for the children. Another day, they decided to have a big picnic beside the river. The children brought tasty food, fresh fruit, and cool drinks and spread them out beside the river. They ate together, they played, and they enjoyed the beautiful wild world.

On one of those days, the children thought of building a tree house. Working together and using strong wood, they built a beautiful, sturdy tree house. This tree house became their special meeting place, and every day they went there to play and tell one another new stories.

Summer for the children was full of happy moments and beautiful memories. They learned that with working together and friendship they could make anything possible and enjoy every moment. As autumn came near and school opened again, the children went back to school with hearts full of summer memories, and they always remembered those adventures and happy summer games.

From then on, every summer, Arman, Melika, Artin, and Leila made new plans together and tried new adventures. They always learned that friendship and working together are the most important treasure in life, and they always kept this treasure in their hearts.

The End.
