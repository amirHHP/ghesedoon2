Lambie was a young, clever sheep who lived on a little farm. With his soft white wool and his bright eyes, he was always looking for adventure and new discoveries. Lambie’s farm was full of green grasses and fruit trees that gave a pleasant color and smell to all the creatures.

One beautiful day, Lambie decided to go on an adventure on the farm. With a smile full of joy and energy, he told his sheep friends, including the ewes and the goats, and everyone welcomed him happily and wished him well.

Lambie’s first job was to go looking for tasty fruits on the farm. By wandering and walking among the many kinds of plants, he found colorful fruits like strawberries and sour cherries and ate them with pleasure. Then Lambie went to a flowing river on the farm and drank the clear, fresh water that came down from the mountain, and he got his energy back.

Next, Lambie joined his friends in a high-speed race of the birds and animals of the farm. He raced with the ewes and the goats in a race full of speed and joy, and he always tried to be first. These races reminded everyone that with friendship and teamwork, they can reach their goals and share happy moments together—just like Lambie, the young sheep in this story.

The End.
