On a sunny, happy day, in a small, cozy house, a new doll named Pico came into the world. She was a small, colorful doll who had just joined a family of lovable dolls. Pico had newly arrived from the same factory where all the house dolls had come from.

Pico took her place among her family with a smile full of joy. Every day she played and went on adventures with her new friends, Jino and Lulu. Together they played in the house, put on concerts, and listened to exciting stories.

One day, the doll family decided to hold a show of their favorite games. Every doll had to present their very best game. Pico and her friends looked for an idea for their own game.

Pico and her friends came up with a lovely idea: a treasure hunt. They searched for treasure in their little house, found hiding places, and excitedly looked for hidden treasures in different rooms. Their game was so much fun that it drew in the whole doll family.

When the show ended, everyone went home and enjoyed their games and everyday adventures. Pico smiled from the corner and told her new family about the happy moments they had shared: “I will always have good memories of you!”

This story reminds children that with friendship and togetherness they can have a happy day full of adventure every day — just like Pico and her friends in this story.

The End.
