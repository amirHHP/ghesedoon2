In a faraway world, there was a big, delightful forest full of the beauties of nature. This forest was home to many plants and animals who lived there together. From tall trees and green leaves to colorful flowers and little plants, each one had a part in keeping the rich life of the forest.

One day, news spread through the forest that a festival of plant variety would be held. All the plants and animals from all over the forest were invited to take part. Everyone gathered in the forest together: the big trees with branches full of leaves, the beautiful flowers of many colors, the little plants under the cover of the leaves, and even the tiny mushrooms under the trees.

The first contest at the festival of plant variety was the beautiful-leaf contest. All the plants tried to show their best and most beautiful leaves. The trees with big green leaves, the flowers with colorful petals, and the little plants with mustard-colored leaves—each one was beautiful in its own way.

The second contest was the sweet-scent contest of the flowers. Each flower tried to have a pleasant, lovely smell so the festival would be full of flower perfume. From the fragrant flowers to the sweet-smelling ones, each added its best scent to the festival.

The next contest was the wind-music contest. The winds passed among the plants and flowers, and together they made natural, sweet melodies. The leaves trembled, the flowers were stroked by the breeze, and the little plants moved with joy—all answering the caress of the wind.

At the end, the festival finished with a storytelling contest and a dance of the flowers. The flowers, with their colors and petals, told beautiful stories of life in the forest, and each one did its most beautiful dance.

When the festival ended, all the plants and animals shook hands and had happy laughs on their lips. They congratulated one another and thanked each other for helping keep the variety and beauty of the forest.

The forest, with its many kinds of plants, was a rich home for everyone. Every living thing in the forest could enjoy the beauties and treasures of the forest, and by working together and being kind, they could keep these beauties safe.

This story reminds children that every creature in nature, each with its own beauty and specialness, plays a part in keeping the variety of life, and that they respect one another so they can help each other and make life better and more beautiful.

The End.
