Once upon a time, in a faraway countryside, there lived a little boy named Arya. Arya was a boy who always loved the night sky and the stars. Every night, before sleep, he went to the window of his room and looked at the dark sky. He always wondered how the stars shone in the sky, and it seemed as if they were giving light.

One day, Arya found a book in the family library and met the world of galaxies. In the book there were pictures of galaxies, stars, and wonders of the sky that made everything clearer. Arya grew excited when he saw these pictures, and he decided that one day he would go discovering galaxies.

Every night he searched the sky for stars and galaxies. With every star that shone, he imagined that maybe one of those galaxies was looking for him right then. With every new galaxy he had seen in the book, Arya made friends and gave it a name.

One night, Arya looked at the sky and found a new galaxy he had not seen in the books. This galaxy shone in the sky in a magical, wonderful way. Arya was happy and amazed, and he named this galaxy the Blue Galaxy, because its color was like the blue of the sea.

Arya decided to tell his friends the story of the Blue Galaxy. He told everyone how, on a dark night, he had discovered the Blue Galaxy and how he enjoyed it. Arya's friends were all so happy when they saw pictures of the sky and heard his story, and they decided to look at the night sky to discover new galaxies they had not seen yet.

Arya went on discovering galaxies with his friends every night and introduced everyone to the magical, endless world of galaxies and the sky. He knew that the sky and the galaxies are always a wonderful, surprising world to discover, and that these discoveries, every day and every night, would keep Arya and his friends amazed by a world full of mystery and wonder forever.

The End.
