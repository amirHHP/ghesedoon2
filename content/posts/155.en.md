In a small, pretty village, there lived a boy named Arman. Arman was a curious, clever boy who always wanted to learn new things and make discoveries. He lived in a house with a big garden full of colorful flowers and tall trees.

One day, while Arman was playing in the garden, he saw something strange. A big, old box sat under one of the trees. Arman ran to the box with excitement and opened it. Inside were many science tools: a microscope, glass jars, a dropper, and a little book with all kinds of instructions.

Arman thought happily that this box was a real treasure. He carried the tools into the house and decided to set up a little science lab in his own room. He lined everything up neatly on the table and got ready for his first experiment.

Arman’s first experiment was making a rainbow from different liquids. He opened the little book and read the instructions. Carefully, he picked up the dropper and began mixing the colorful liquids. To his surprise, when he mixed them together, beautiful, wonderful colors appeared.

Arman said with excitement, “Wow, this is so interesting! I can make new colors!”

After the rainbow experiment worked, Arman tried another one. He decided to use the microscope to discover the hidden world under the lens. He took a drop of water from the river near his house and put it under the microscope. When he looked inside, he was amazed to see tiny, strange creatures moving around in just one drop of water.

Arman said in surprise, “Wow, where were these little creatures? I never thought there was so much life inside one drop of water!”

Arman decided to share these interesting discoveries with his friends. He invited Sara and Mehdi to his house and took them to his little lab. Sara and Mehdi were very excited when they saw the science tools and Arman’s discoveries.

Arman said to his friends, “Let’s do more experiments together and discover new things!”

They began doing all kinds of experiments together. One of them was making a little volcano. Using vinegar and baking soda, they made a small volcano that erupted with foam and amazed everyone.

Sara laughed and said, “That was so interesting! I always wanted to see a volcano.”

Mehdi added, “Wow, I never thought I could make a volcano myself!”

The days passed, and Arman and his friends did new experiments every day and learned new things. They found out that science and scientific discoveries can be very interesting and exciting, and that they help us know the world around us better.

One day, Arman said to his friends, “I think science is very important. With science, we can solve our problems and make a better world.”

Sara and Mehdi nodded and said, “Yes, we think so too. Let’s always stay curious and keep learning.”

Arman and his friends decided to set aside one day every week for science experiments and new discoveries. They studied together, did new experiments, and learned more and more every day.

And that is how the story of Arman and his science lab came to an end. But it was only the beginning of their science adventures. Arman and his friends learned that science never finishes, and there are always new things to discover and learn. They kept going together, so that every day they could learn more about the world around them and help make a better world.

The End.
