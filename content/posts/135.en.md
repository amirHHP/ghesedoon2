In a faraway village there lived a little boy named Artin. Artin was a boy with a kind heart and a cheerful spirit. He was always looking for new adventures around the village, and every day he walked the forests and meadows nearby with his friends.

One day Artin came upon a meadow that shone like sunlight in the middle of the fields, and there stood a beautiful white horse. He stepped closer in wonder, and the horse looked at Artin with wise eyes. Artin felt that this horse was more than an animal. This horse was a loyal friend.

Artin slowly held out his hand and came closer with a loving feeling. The horse stood nearer still and called to Artin with a high, gentle sound, asking for kindness. Artin was so happy, and he and the dreamy white horse became friends with ease.

From then on, Artin and the white horse shared a close friendship. They crossed the forests together, wandered through the meadows, and even sat beside the rivers. Artin named the white horse Snowy, inspired by that white, shining coat. This friendship gave Artin peace and joy, and they never stayed far apart.

Snowy was not only Artin's friend. He was also a companion and a helper. Whenever Artin felt sad or low, Snowy would invite him to learn and play with a wise little nicker or a happy, lively move. Artin cared for Snowy just as much, and he used all his own gifts to grow this special friendship.

And that is how the friendship between Artin and the white horse stayed strong, always sharing joy and calm with everyone around them.

The End.
