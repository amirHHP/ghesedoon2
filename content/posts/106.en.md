Near a small and quiet village, where the mountains and the sea met, there lived a child named Arash. Arash woke up happily every day and went to the sea. He loved the sea and felt that the sea was talking to him.

One morning, when Arash went to the beach, he saw that the sea was very calm. The waves were coming to the shore slowly and it seemed that the sea wanted to talk to Arash. Arash went to the nearest rock and sat down. "Hello sea! How are you today?" he called out.

"Hi Arash! I'm fine, how are you?" Darya replied in a soft voice.

Arash answered excitedly: "I'm very good! I'd like to play with you today."

"Okay, let's play together," the sea said with a laugh. "You can build little boats and I'll send them little waves so they can move on the water."

Arash happily went to the beach and built small boats using sticks and leaves. He put the boats on the water and watched the gentle waves of the sea move them. Arash was very happy playing with his boats and felt that the sea was his best friend.

A few days later, Arash found an empty bottle. He decided to write a letter to the sea. He wrote in his letter: "My dear Sea, thank you for playing with me and being such a good friend. I always like to come next to you and talk to you. Love you, Arash."

Arash put the letter in the bottle and threw the bottle into the water. He watched with excitement as the bottle floated slowly on the water and went off into the distance.

The next day, when Arash went to the beach, he saw a big oyster by the water. He picked up the shell and found a letter inside. The letter was from Daria and it was written: "Dear Arash, I am also very happy to be friends with you. I am always waiting for you to come to my side and play together. Love you, Daria."

Arash was very happy to read the letter. He put the oyster in his pocket and looked at the sea with a smile. He knew that the sea is always his friend and whenever he comes to the beach, the sea is waiting for him to play together and have a good time.

From that day on, Arash and Darya spent time together every day. They played together, talked and enjoyed their friendship. Arash happily went to the sea every day and knew that the sea was his best friend.

And thus, Arash and Darya had a friendship that always remained in Arash's heart. Every time he looked at the sea, a smile appeared on his lips and he remembered his friendship with the sea. A friend who showed him that even the sea can be a child's best friend.

The End.
