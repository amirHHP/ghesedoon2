In a faraway, beautiful forest, little insects lived. These insects included butterflies with colorful wings, little pigeons with sweet voices, and little worms who were always friends and shared their lives together.

One day, an unkind wizard came to the forest and, with a strange command, filled all the insects with surprise. He had a power that let him catch for himself any insect he looked at.

The butterflies and the pigeons and even the worms became very worried. They did not know what to do. They tried to run from the wizard, but they had no luck.

But there was a little worm named Wormy who had courage and a brave heart. He decided to look for a way to save his friends. He said to all the insects, "We must not give up. We must work together and stop the wizard."

All the insects joined together. The butterflies caught his eye with their wings, the pigeons made him uneasy with their voices, and the worms moved quickly around the forest to trick the wizard and stop him.

When the wizard came to catch the insects, he said in a loud voice, "I have magic power, and you cannot run from this magic!" But the insects, with their courage and strong choice, played with the wizard. They showed him that only with one another and togetherness can they solve any problem.

At last, the wizard saw with surprise that the insects had not only stopped him, but had shown him that everything is better together. He gave up and left the forest.

All the insects were happy together and enjoyed their good life in the forest. They understood that everyone has a power that can help others, and that togetherness and kindness always win.

This was a story of insects and magic that reminds everyone that together they can always do well and solve problems.

The End.
