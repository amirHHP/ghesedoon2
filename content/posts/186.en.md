In a small, quiet village, life was gentle and pleasant. In one of the houses in this village, a young woman named Anna lived with her little boy, Mark. Anna was a kind mother who always sang to Mark and loved him.

At night, Anna tucked Mark into bed and sang him a lullaby. Anna’s lullabies were like a soothing song for Mark, and they carried him into a deep, sweet sleep. Mark always drifted off while he listened to the beautiful stories and adventures his mother told him.

Once, when Mark was very tired and could not fall asleep on his own, Anna told him a story about a magic bird who was searching for treasure. The story was about faraway lands, princes, and dragons, and it carried Mark into a new and wonderful world.

Every night, before Mark fell asleep, Anna told him stories from a magic world or adventures about friendship among the animals of the forest. These stories not only entertained Mark, they also gave him a feeling of safety and calm, knowing that Mama was beside him and always would be.

In this village, Anna and Mark’s stories always reached other ears too. Their neighbors and friends always welcomed Anna’s lullabies for their own children and enjoyed the sweet stories Anna made for Mark.

That is why, every night in the village, peace settled in while Anna sang a lullaby to Mark. The stories Anna told him helped every child have a calm, deep sleep and fall asleep with a feeling of safety and rest.

The End.
