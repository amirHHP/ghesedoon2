In a small, delightful town, the life of a child named Alice flowed with her family, full of happiness and fun. Alice was a little girl with bright eyes and lots of energy who was always looking to discover new and interesting things. She loved animals, and especially cats—especially a little yellow cat named Susan.

Susan was a special cat, because she could talk! She spoke in human language and talked with Alice and her family. This clever, lovable cat always talked with Alice about their everyday adventures and went along with her.

One day, Alice and Susan decided to go on a new adventure. They went to a garden full of beautiful flowers and fruit trees. Alice, with happy, playful movements, hid behind the fruit trees, and Susan went with her and followed her. Together, among the hanging flowers and the songs of the birds, they were busy with a lovely search.

As soon as Alice and Susan reached a pile of wood, a strange and interesting adventure happened. A big gray cat named Vincent came out from the woodpile and spoke with Alice and Susan. Vincent was a lovable cat who, like Susan, could talk, and he talked with children about their lives and adventures.

Alice and Susan talked with Vincent about him, his home, and his friends. Vincent told them beautiful stories of his adventures in the forest and the garden, and that made him even more lovable than before. Alice listened to Vincent’s stories with happiness and joy, and Susan listened to her friend’s words with care and excitement.

As time passed, Alice, Susan, and Vincent returned home. Together they had a memory of this unforgettable day full of talk and stories, and they always thought of it as one of the best days of their lives. Alice’s friendship with the special talking cats showed that with energy and by choosing new friends well, you can go on lovely adventures and always enjoy life with pleasure and joy.

The End.
