Once upon a time, in an age of adventure and discovery, a little girl named Leila was on a road trip with her mother and father. They were looking for a lost island known as the Lost Island, said to lie in the deep seas.

Leila looked far away through the car window with wide eyes. She wanted so much to search for the Lost Island and, by seeing it, have an exciting adventure. She listened to all the stories her father told about mysterious islands, and she always hoped that one day she would travel to a real island.

Leila’s mother and father liked the idea too. They had brought maps and books of seas and islands, and they looked for every sign of the Lost Island. But so far none of those tries had come to anything.

One day, when they stood beside a shore full of waves and big rocks, Leila found an old wooden box next to one of the stones. The box looked very old and had strange patterns and pictures. Leila opened the box carefully and found several old maps and an old letter inside.

The letter, written by an old jeweler, talked about a mysterious island with adventurous curiosity. He explained that the island was hidden and far away, and that a great deal of treasure was hidden there. But to reach it, they needed to find a special point on the maps.

With her mother and father, Leila studied the maps carefully and found a point known as the Golden Spot. That point was on a faraway seashore that could be a gate to the Lost Island.

Guided by the maps, Leila and her family went looking for the Golden Spot. On a journey full of adventure, big rocks, and blue waves, they kept following this point. As they came nearer to the shores, Leila felt that at this very moment they were drawing close to their Lost Island.

When the sun reached its highest place, they at last found the Golden Spot. There they saw a calm lake and beautiful gardens with shining flowers and green trees. This beautiful island truly was the Lost Island they had been looking for.

Leila and her family stepped gently onto the island and felt as if they had come home. They celebrated on this island and enjoyed its beauty and peace. Leila always remembered that with trust in an old map and belief in yourself, you can go anywhere your heart wishes and live a life of endless adventure.

The End.
