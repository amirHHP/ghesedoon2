In a deep blue sea there lived a young fish named Rainbow. He was a very special fish, because he had all the colors of the rainbow on his body. He had every color you could think of: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and even gray. Every day, with the sunlight, he went to the waters around him and, with his lovely colors, made a bright spot in those waters.

Rainbow loved to swim all around the sea and make friends with the other fish. He made friends with many fish whose colors were ordinary, and he always helped them. Rainbow knew that he was known as a special and different fish, but he still loved to be friends with everyone.

One day, Rainbow met a young fish named Bubbleback. Bubbleback was an ordinary fish with ordinary colors, but he was very cheerful and lovable. He always helped Rainbow and his friends, and he never had trouble with anyone.

Rainbow and Bubbleback soon became good friends and explored the sea together. They went to faraway islands and made friends with other fish. Rainbow showed Bubbleback how to find the very best little bits of food for small fish, and Bubbleback learned from Rainbow how to meet the challenges of the sea with a brave heart.

Their friendship became known in every way, and it always stayed in those beautiful waters.

The End.
