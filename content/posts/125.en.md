In a faraway forest full of secrets, there lived a big giant named Ghariba. Ghariba was a big giant with a good heart, but he was always alone and had no friend. Beside a tall tree covered with green leaves, he hid himself among the deep places of the forest. All the creatures of the forest were afraid of him and called him a bad giant, but in truth Ghariba was only a lonely giant with a tired heart.

One day, a little child named Arya came to the forest with her family. Arya was a curious, brave girl who always loved to wander the forest for new discoveries. Arya was looking for beautiful flowers and colorful birds that flew from the sky and twirled the bright leaves of the forest.

When Arya went under a big, wonderful tree, Ghariba suddenly appeared in front of her. For the first time in his life, Ghariba grew close to a child. Arya was not afraid of the giant at all. She trusted him and talked about the beauty of the forest. She told the giant how beautiful it was here, and how you can be friends with the trees and the animals of the forest.

Ghariba also told Arya about his own world. He told Arya how he lived alone in the forest and how much he wanted to be close to someone else. Arya also showed the giant how you can take care of nature and grow close to the animals of the forest.

From that day, Ghariba and Arya found a beautiful, sudden friendship. Arya came to the forest every day to play with the big giant and talk with him about the secrets of the woods. Ghariba taught Arya how we should look at the flowers and the trees with loving eyes and take care of the forest.

This friendship showed that sometimes the things that seem farthest away are in truth the closest things. This story not only taught Arya and Ghariba that they should always find new friends, but taught everyone how to grow close to one another and enjoy the beauties of nature.

The End.
