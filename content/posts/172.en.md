Welcome to a summer day in a little village full of warm, sunny weather! In these hot summer days, there was a beautiful life in this village. The children played happily in the streets and gardens, the trees made tasty fruits, and everyone was looking for something cool and fresh for themselves.

In these hot summer days, a big, juicy watermelon sat in a garden. This watermelon was as big as a bed, and it had a green, crisp rind that shone with freshness in the warm summer sun. The children of the village called it “the big summer watermelon,” and they were proud to have a watermelon so big and tasty.

One day, on a hot summer afternoon, the children of the village decided to take the big summer watermelon and eat it at a lovely, happy picnic in the garden. Together they got all the tools they needed ready: a mallet, a blade, and they were ready to scratch the green, unripe rind.

The children came close to the watermelon with joy and began to work. Each one, in turn, slowly and carefully scratched the green rind with the mallet and helped the watermelon open. With all this work, the big, juicy, tasty summer watermelon appeared, and the children enjoyed it very much.

With the green rind set aside, they all, with eyes full of joy and little bits of melon, enjoyed eating the fresh summer watermelon. That day, they would always remember the big, tasty watermelon, along with happy, joyful moments, forever.

The End.
