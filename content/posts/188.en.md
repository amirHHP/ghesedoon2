Once upon a time, in a small village, the sky filled every day with a rainbow of sunlight and rain. In this village, life was beautiful and full of the color and scent of joy. In Princess Lily’s house, the days always began with a rainbow dance.

Lily was a little girl with big eyes the color of the blue sky. She always looked at the world with a bright, happy smile, and every day she waited with all her energy for the rainbow dance in the sky.

One beautiful morning, the sky filled with a rainbow. When Princess Lily saw this lovely sight, she woke with joy and began to dance. Whenever the sunlight came out from behind the clouds and an arc of rainbow appeared in the sky, she filled with excitement.

Lily loved to move under the rainbows and jump back and forth. Dancing under those rainbows was a magic moment for her. With every step she took, the rainbows seemed to carry her into a new and wonderful world.

One of those days, Lily received an invitation from Mr. Rainbow. The invitation said: “Dear Lily, you are invited to the Festival of Rainbows! Come so we can dress the rainbows for the celebration and rise into the sky together.”

Lily went to the Festival of Rainbows with her friends, full of happiness and excitement. There, all the children danced and jumped together under a sky full of rainbows. Each one pictured the rainbows in their own special way and flew under the sky like colorful butterflies.

At the end of the festival, all the children invited their parents to see their rainbows. Everyone looked at this art of nature with a smile and enjoyed it.

After the festival, Lily went home and fell into her mother’s arms. She told her mother, “Mama, today I had the biggest rainbow dance! All my friends and I danced under a calm, pleasant sky.”

Lily’s mother hugged her with a tender smile and said, “My dear, rainbows always remind us of the beauty and joy of a world full of color and happiness. Every day, go to your rainbow dance with joy and energy, and enjoy life.”

Lily listened to her mother with a big, happy smile and said, “Yes, Mama. I will always think of these rainbows, and with my own energy I will always dance.”

And from that day on, Lily danced under the rainbows every day, and she always remembered that every rainbow showed her a new and wonderful world.

The End.
