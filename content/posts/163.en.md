On a calm, beautiful shore, where the blue water sparkled because of the sunlight, a colorful group of birds lived. These birds, each with their own color, shape, and ways, spent their lives on that shore in a charming, wonderful way.

One of the birds of this shore was named Blackbeak. He was a big black bird with white wings who, every morning, flew from a tree near the shore and looked for food. Blackbeak loved to fly freely in the blue sky and over the trembling waves of the sea, and to play with his bird friends.

Another of the birds of this shore was named Goldie. He was a little bird with colorful wings and a long tail who always sat on the branches of the trees near the shore. Goldie loved to come down from the tops of the trees to the ground and, standing in the bright sun, gently flap his wings.

Other birds lived on this shore too, including the little nurse birds who always searched for food with calm, careful touches, and sea birds like the seagulls who whirled over the blue waves with their beautiful, fast wings.

One day, all the birds gathered for the yearly festival of life on the shore. This festival was a chance for everyone to be happy together and enjoy life on this beautiful shore. At this festival, each bird took part in different contests with their own glory and beauty. Blackbeak with his flight from high above, Goldie with his dance, and the little nurse birds with their happy, careful search for food—each played their part in the best way.

At the end of the festival, all the birds joined a contest whose goal was to gather trash from the sea. With teamwork and kindness, they picked up the trash from the shore, and in this way they helped keep the shore clean and beautiful.

This story reminds children that every bird in nature has its own part, and that by working together and being kind, they can help take care of the world around them and the beauties of nature. It also shows them that all birds, with their own special variety and beauty, are part of the beautiful world of nature, and that they should be treated with respect and love.

The End.
