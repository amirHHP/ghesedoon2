In a small village, where families lived with warmth and closeness, there lived an energetic little boy named Arman. Arman was always looking for new adventures, and he loved nature and the sky very much.

One night, while Arman was looking at the sky, a strange, shining thing came down from the sky to the earth. With wide eyes he looked at the space creature that appeared from behind a big tree. This creature was a space whale, and it had a sweet, friendly voice.

The space creature smiled at Arman and said, "Hello! I come from a faraway planet called Brightbeauty. Who are you?"

Arman looked at the space creature with excitement and said, "I am Arman! I am so glad to meet you. Where did you come from?"

The space whale told Arman that it had come from a faraway planet called Brightbeauty and that it was looking for new adventures. Arman also told the space whale about his village and his friends and everything he loved. Soon Arman and the space whale became close friends and spent happy times together.

From then on, every night Arman and the space whale looked at the stars and the sky together and shared beautiful stories. With its power, the space whale took Arman on new adventures, like journeys to other planets and life in space, and Arman listened to every story with care and excitement.

Arman and the space whale were endlessly happy, creative friends who, every night and every day, lived their good friendship on the wide lands with the sky at their backs.

The End.
