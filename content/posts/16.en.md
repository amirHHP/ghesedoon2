In a small, beautiful city called Rainbow, there lived a little boy named Saman. Saman was a creative, energetic boy who loved to paint. Every day after school, he went to his room and painted with his pencils and colors. The walls of Saman's room were full of beautiful, colorful paintings.

One day, Saman's grandmother came to visit him and gave him an old box as a gift. Saman opened the box with excitement and found a few paintbrushes and a few tubes of paint inside. Grandmother said with a smile, "These brushes and paints are magic, Saman. With them you can paint pictures that come to life."

Saman looked at Grandmother in surprise and asked, "Really? How is that possible?" Grandmother went on, "You must paint with care and love. When your painting is finished, look at it and make a wish. Then the painting will come to life."

Saman decided to try. He took a big sheet of paper and began to paint. First he painted a beautiful butterfly, with colorful, shining wings. Then he painted a garden full of colorful flowers and green trees. He drew every detail with care and love. When his painting was finished, he looked at the beautiful butterfly and wished, "I wish this butterfly were alive and could fly in the garden."

Suddenly the painting began to shine, and the butterfly left the paper and flew into the air. Saman looked at the butterfly flying in his room with wide eyes and a big smile. He said happily to Grandmother, "Grandmother, my painting came to life!"

Grandmother said with a smile, "I knew you could. Now you can build a world of magic and wonder with your paintings."

Every day Saman painted new pictures with his magic brushes. One day he painted a big tree full of tasty fruit and wished that the fruit would become real and alive. The fruit left the painted tree, and Saman was able to eat them. Another day he painted a beautiful white horse and wished that the horse would come to life. The horse came out of the painting, and Saman went on make-believe journeys with it.

Saman also found new friends with his magic paintings. One day he painted a little blue bird and wished that it would come to life. The bird came to life and became Saman's best friend. He always played with his bird, and together they went on all kinds of adventures.

One day, Saman decided to paint a big, special picture. He painted a beautiful lake with blue, clear water, and beside it he painted a boat. He wished that he could paddle on the lake in the boat. Suddenly the painting came to life, and Saman found himself beside the lake. He went to the boat with his bird and began to paddle. They floated on the lake full of colorful fish and enjoyed the beauty of nature.

With his magic paintings, Saman was able to build a world of wonders and make-believe adventures. He learned that with creativity and love, you can build a world of magic and wonders. And from that day on, Saman painted every day and turned his wishes into reality with his magic brushes.

In the city of Rainbow, everyone talked about Saman's magic paintings and knew him as a boy with a heart full of creativity and love. And so Saman, with his magic paintings, always made everyone's heart happy and brought a world of beauties.

The End.
