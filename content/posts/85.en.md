In a faraway land, in a kingdom full of trees and colorful flowers, there lived Princess Anika. Anika was a princess with blue eyes and golden hair who always looked at the world with a smile. She helped everyone, and her friends loved her courage and her kind face very much.

One day, news reached Anika from a little town near her kingdom. In that town there lived a terrible monster that slipped into the houses every night and frightened the people. When Anika heard that no one had yet dared to stand up to the monster, she decided to help them.

Princess Anika set off toward the town with a brave heart. She talked with the people of the town and learned about the monster's powers. After that, Anika decided to be ready at night to meet the monster.

On a night when the moonlight was bright, Anika sat under a tree and waited quietly. Suddenly the monster appeared. He was big, rough, and scary, but Anika stepped forward with courage and said to the monster, "You must leave this place. You cannot frighten these people."

The monster laughed with a scary voice and said, "Who are you to tell me what to do?"

Anika answered with a stronger voice, "I am Princess Anika, and I am not afraid of you!"

The monster, surprised by Anika's courage, began to grow afraid of her, and at last he decided never to go to the town again. The people of the town, truly moved by Anika's courage, honored her and thanked her.

Anika went back among the people of the town with a smile, and everyone congratulated her together. From then on, Princess Anika was known not only as a beautiful, kind princess, but also as a brave princess and a hero.

The End.
