On a calm, quiet night, in a small, cozy house, there lived a special pillow named Cottonpaw. He was a sleepy pillow of sweet dreams who went on adventures in the world of dreams every night with his friends — lovely little children.

With his soft blanket and his own little colorful canopy, Cottonpaw found his way into a magic world each night. He always waited for the little children named Sara, Ali, and Niloufar to come into the room so they could have sweet dreams together.

One night, Sara came home from school, tired and sleepy. She smiled at Cottonpaw and said, “Dear Cottonpaw, what dreams will we see tonight?” Cottonpaw smiled and said, “Come along — I have been waiting for you!”

After all the children had gathered in the room, Cottonpaw sent a flame of rainbow light from the top of his little canopy and told them, “Dreaming is just for you. I will give you a path into the world of sweet dreams.” Then, with a little magic, he carried them all into the dream world.

In the world of dreams they went on endless adventures. They joined colorful birds and sweet-smelling flowers, magic toys, and a world of wishes and hopes. Each child found a special world of their own in those dreams.

At sunrise they returned to their room, and each of them thought about the sweet adventures and new friendships they had found in the dream world. Cottonpaw smiled and said, “May your dreams always be sweet, and I will always be here for you, so you can travel into your world of dreams.”

This story reminds children that with imagination and friendship with the things around them, they can travel into a world of dreams and sweet adventures — just like the children in the story of Cottonpaw and his friends.

The End.
