Once upon a time, in a little village in the heart of the mountains, there stood a dark, mysterious castle. With its tall stone walls and windows that were always closed, this castle stayed a puzzle and a secret for the children of the village.

One day, a little boy named Arman, whose heart longed to discover the secrets of the dark castle, decided to find out about this mysterious land. Arman asked his friends Melika, Artin, and Leila for help, and they all began to look around the castle together.

They walked through the village streets and looked in every corner, but they found no secret. Even though the castle windows were closed, they wanted very much to know what was happening inside.

One moonlit night, when the moon shone very brightly in the sky, they decided to go to the dark castle. They looked slowly and carefully at the stone walls of the castle and felt they had never seen anything like it. At that moment, a strange, unknown sound was heard from inside the castle.

Arman and his friends all huddled together, a little scared and a little excited. Then they held hands and set off.

The End.
