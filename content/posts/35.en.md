In a small, beautiful town lived a boy named Arad. Arad was a curious, energetic boy who was always looking for new adventures. He loved wonderful stories full of secrets, and he always wished that one day he himself would have a special, magical experience.

One day Arad was playing in their garden when suddenly his eye fell on a little shiny train. This strange train sat in a corner of the garden that Arad had never seen. Arad went toward the train and looked at it carefully. On the body of the train was written: “Time Train.”

Arad said to himself with excitement, “Time Train? What does that mean? Maybe with this train I can travel to the past or the future!”

With both doubt and excitement, Arad went into the train. Inside it was full of colorful buttons and lights. Suddenly a kind, sweet voice reached his ears: “Hello, Arad! Welcome to the Time Train. You can travel to any time you like. You only have to press the right button.”

Arad smiled and said, “Hello! This is so exciting. First I would like to travel to the past and see what my grandfather was like as a child.”

Arad pressed a button marked “Past,” and suddenly everything spun around him. When the spinning stopped, Arad found himself in an old village. He looked around and suddenly saw a boy who looked very much like his grandfather, but much younger.

Arad came close to the boy and said, “Hello! I’m Arad. Who are you?”

The boy smiled and said, “Hello, Arad! I’m Mehrdad. Do you want to play together?”

Arad said happily, “Yes! Let’s play together.”

Arad and Mehrdad played all day and had many adventures. Arad learned that his grandfather had also been very brave and kind as a child. When the day ended, Arad said goodbye to Mehrdad and went back to the Time Train.

Arad said to himself, “How interesting that was! Now I want to travel to the future and see what our world will be like.”

He pressed a button marked “Future,” and everything spun around him again. When the spinning stopped, Arad found himself in a world full of strange new machines. The buildings were taller and more modern, and cars flew in the air.

Arad looked around in wonder and met a boy named Ario. Ario smiled and said, “Hello! I’m Ario. Which time have you come from?”

Arad said in surprise, “Hello, Ario! I have come from the past. Your world is so interesting!”

Ario said with pride, “Yes, we have come a long way. Come, and I will take you to the sights.”

Ario took Arad to a school where robots taught the children. They went to a park where children played with robot animals, and to a museum that showed the history of machines. Arad enjoyed seeing all these new, exciting things very much.

When the day ended, Arad said goodbye to Ario and went back to the Time Train. He thought of his adventures in the past and the future and said to himself, “What wonderful journeys those were! Now I treasure the present more than ever.”

Arad pressed a button marked “Return Home” and went back to his own house. He told his family the stories of his adventures, and they listened in wonder. Arad learned that it does not matter which time you are in. What matters is to enjoy every moment and meet new friends.

From that day on, Arad always remembered that life is full of adventure and wonder, and that every moment that passes is a chance to discover new things and learn. He looked at the future with a smile and knew that wherever he went, new friends and wonderful adventures would always be waiting for him.

The End.
