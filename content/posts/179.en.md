In a greenhouse full of colorful flowers and sweet scents lived a dragonfly named Shayan. He was a very bright, shining dragonfly, with a red-and-yellow body and glossy, clear wings. Shayan always hopped from flower to flower with a smile on his face, resting here and there, and he loved his life in the greenhouse.

One beautiful day, Shayan decided to look for new friends. He flew around the greenhouse with his wings, and at last he flew into a big garden nearby. There he met new friends: a dove with soft white wings, a little bee with a black body and bright yellow spots, and a lovely butterfly with colorful yellow wings.

Shayan and his friends had so much fun together. They flew from flower to flower, enjoyed the wonderful smell of the blossoms, and watched the sunshine sparkle on their wings. Life in the garden was always full of adventure and delight for them.

One day, while they were flying and playing, a sudden rain began. Drops of water fell from the sky in all the colors of the rainbow and touched these beautiful friends. They quickly looked for shelter among the trees and kept safe together under the cover of the leaves.

After the rain, Shayan and his friends went back to flying and playing. They understood that they lived in a world of friendship and kindness, where they always stayed beside one another and helped each other when things were hard.

The story of Shayan the dragonfly reminds children that with new friends they can share happy moments, and that they can always stand together when times are hard — just like Shayan and his friends in this story.

The End.
