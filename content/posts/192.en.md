Once upon a time, in a little town, it was early morning, and the sun was slowly coming out from behind the mountain. In the houses everyone had woken up, and the air was cool, with a good breeze full of the scent of new flowers.

Inside a small house, Leila and her friends were getting ready for a tasty breakfast. Leila was a little, cheerful girl, with eyes full of the beauty of nature and a smiling face. She was always off on an adventure, and she loved to spend a good time with her friends.

Leila’s friends were all kind, well-mannered girls. Maria had long brown hair, Amahl had green hazel eyes, and Anna had yellow hair and wandering feet. They all sat together in the place where a tasty breakfast was waiting for them.

The mothers had made a delicious breakfast for them. The table was full of fresh bread and warm milk, jam and honey. All of this was set on the table, and a tasty smell floated around it.

Leila and her friends were very hungry. They loved sitting together and eating breakfast. Each of them enjoyed the fresh bread and jam and drank the warm milk. During breakfast they shared sweet stories and thought of their memories of days full of laughter and play.

Breakfast for Leila’s friends was more than a meal. It was a time when they grew closer, found new friendships, and always stayed side by side. Every day, with a tasty breakfast, their friendship grew stronger, and they had happy times together.

At the end of breakfast they left the table with smiles and laughter, ready for the new, exciting adventures waiting for them. Every day, with a tasty breakfast and friends, a new day began for Leila and her friends, happier than the day before.

The End.
