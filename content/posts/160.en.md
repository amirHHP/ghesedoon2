In a small village by the sea, there lived a little boy named Arash. Arash was a happy, cheerful boy who always liked to look at the blue sky and think about flying in airplanes. Every morning he woke up early and looked at the sky with eagerness, wishing that one day he would travel through the sky with the airplanes.

One day, Arash and his mother went to the airport in the nearby city. When he saw the big, birdlike airplanes landing from the sky and taking off, he became very excited. He said to his mother in wonder, “Mama, how can these airplanes fly up from the ground?”

Arash’s mother smiled and said, “My son, airplanes have big engines that help them fly in the sky. Airplanes are like great birds that, with the help of strong winds, can reach their destination.”

Arash asked his mother eagerly, “Can I go up into the sky in an airplane one day too?”

His mother said calmly, “Yes, my son. Anyone can travel by airplane. To go up into the sky, you should go to school and study so you can learn how an airplane is flown.”

Arash said to his mother with eagerness, “I really want to learn, and one day go up into the sky with the airplanes!”

His mother smiled and said, “My son, if you go to school and study well, you can surely turn this dream into something real.”

Every week Arash and his mother went to the airport and looked through the windows at the airplanes flying in the blue sky. Arash watched the airplanes carefully and imagined that he too was flying in the blue sky and traveling to a faraway place.

One day, Arash said to his mother, “Mama, I am ready now to go up into the sky in an airplane. I have studied a lot and learned everything.”

His mother smiled and said, “That is wonderful, my dear. Now we can buy an airplane ticket and go on an exciting trip.”

Arash left the house happily and went to the airport. When he saw the big, splendid airplanes spreading clouds through the sky, he felt glad and waited for his turn to fly.

When the airplane was ready to take off, Arash went inside with joy and excitement. He looked out the window, and when he saw the ground growing farther away, his happiness rose to its highest. The airplane lifted off the ground and flew in the blue sky. Arash looked out the window with excitement and enjoyed the beautiful view of the sky and the little sky-watchers scattered high above.

He flew far from the ground and felt as if he himself were a great bird flying in the blue sky. Arash smiled at his mother and said, “Mama, now I feel like a great bird flying in the blue sky!”

His mother said with a kind smile, “That is good, my dear. These days, flying in the blue sky is a wonderful experience for you. Always remember that whatever you want, you can turn your dreams into something real.”

And that is how Arash flew in the blue sky with the airplane and felt that anything is possible if you have enough hope and will. He always remembered to follow his dreams and wishes, and to enjoy the beauty of the sky.

The End.
