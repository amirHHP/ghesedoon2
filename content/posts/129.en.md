In a faraway forest, where tall, lively trees reached the sky, there lived a very special bird named Zarin. Zarin was a bird with golden, shining feathers, and every child who saw those feathers was filled with wonder.

One day, as Zarin wandered through the forest, he saw a little child sitting sadly under a tree. The child was confused and no longer knew the way out of the forest. Zarin flew quickly toward the child with his golden feathers and asked, "Hello, why are you so sad?"

The child looked at Zarin and said, "I am lost. I do not know where to go or how to get home."

Zarin smiled with the beauty of his feathers and said, "Do not worry! I will help you." Then he took the child gently and flew up into the sky, so he could look over the tops of the trees and search for the path back home.

Zarin's best friend, a happy, energetic rabbit named Pearl, soon came to help them too. Pearl looked at Zarin and the child with laughing eyes and said, "We will find the way soon. Do not worry!"

Zarin, Pearl, and the little child flew through the sky, looking for signs that would lead the child home. Would they find the way back?

In this magical world full of friendship, anything is possible. This story ends here for now, but the beautiful adventures of Zarin, Pearl, and the little child go on and on.

The End.
