In a small, beautiful village, where the flowers always laughed with joy and the blue sky was full of white clouds, there was a little, lovable house. In this house lived a small, warm family. A kind father and mother, and two little children named Milad and Nika.

The neighbors of this family were all kind and good-hearted. Beside their house there was a beautiful house where a lovable old man named Uncle Mohammad lived. Uncle Mohammad was an old man who always lived with a smile on his lips and with love and care for his neighbors. He always worked in his fields and kept his garden tidy.

Uncle Mohammad loved very much to play with Milad and Nika. He always welcomed them and brought them tasty sweets. Milad and Nika always played with Uncle Mohammad too, and together they looked for new things in Uncle Mohammad’s garden.

One of the other neighbors was Mrs. Reza, a young woman full of energy and cheer. She always welcomed her neighbors with a smile on her lips and with kindness. Mrs. Reza always helped Milad and Nika’s family and cooked tasty foods for them.

The other neighbors too, like Mr. Kazem and Mr. Hossein, two men full of good help and kindness, were always ready to help with any need and to share happiness and friendship with all the neighbors.

In this village, the kind neighbors always helped one another and played together in the green, beautiful fields. They always said hello to their neighbors with a smile and were always ready to help one another with any need.

This story reminds children that with a heart full of kindness and cheerfulness, they can make friends with their neighbors and, together on the path of life, feel happiness and joy—just like the kind neighbors of this story.

The End.
