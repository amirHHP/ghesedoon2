In a small, beautiful village lived a little, kind girl named Shirin. Shirin was a clever girl who was always seen with a sweet smile on her lips. That is why everyone called her Sweet Mind. She was always ready to help anyone who needed help.

One day, while Shirin was playing in their garden, she heard a loud cry. She followed the sound and came to a little goat named Bezi who was crying beside the stream. Shirin asked kindly, “Bezi, why are you crying?”

Bezi said in a sad voice, “Oh, Sweet Mind, I cannot go to the other side of the stream because the bridge I always crossed is broken.”

Shirin smiled at Bezi and said, “Don’t worry. I will find a way.” Then she looked around and found long, strong sticks. She carefully placed the sticks side by side and built a temporary bridge. “Now you can cross this bridge, Bezi.”

Bezi crossed the bridge happily and said, “Thank you, Sweet Mind! You are so clever.”

Shirin went on her way until she reached the forest near the village. There she saw a little rabbit named Nani who was digging the ground unhappily. Shirin came close and said, “Nani, what happened?”

Nani sighed and said, “I had hidden my food here, but now I cannot find it.”

Shirin looked around carefully and said, “Don’t worry, Nani. I will help you.” She carefully followed Nani’s footprints and at last came to a spot with fresh soil. “Here it is, Nani! Dig here.”

Nani dug the ground happily and found her food. “Thank you, Sweet Mind! You are my best friend.”

Shirin smiled and went on her way. In the middle of the path she saw a little black crow named Kaki sitting unhappily on a tree. Shirin asked, “Kaki, why are you unhappy?”

Kaki said in a sad voice, “My wing is caught in a tree branch, and I cannot fly.”

Shirin looked at the branch carefully and said, “Don’t worry, Kaki. I will set you free.” She carefully bent the branch gently until Kaki’s wing came free. “Now you can fly, Kaki.”

Kaki flew with joy and said, “Thank you, Sweet Mind! You are so kind and clever.”

Shirin went home and on the way thought about the adventures she had had. She was glad she had helped her friends and knew that with her sweet wisdom and kindness she could solve problems.

At home, Shirin’s grandmother welcomed her with a warm hug and said, “Sweet Mind, what adventures did you have today?”

Shirin smiled and told all her adventures, and Grandma said with pride, “You truly are a clever, kind girl. Always remember that your sweet wisdom and kindness can make the world a better place.”

From that day on, whenever anyone in the village had a problem, they came to Sweet Mind, and she helped everyone with joy. Her friends always learned from her that with sweet wisdom and a kind heart, any problem can be solved. And so it was that Sweet Mind, the little clever girl of the village, became everyone’s hero.

The End.
