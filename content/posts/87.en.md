In a faraway, beautiful forest, an exciting festival full of joy and cheer was held. They called it the Festival of Colors. This festival took place every year in early spring, when the flowers began to dance and the sky shone red, blue, and yellow.

On a sunny day, all the creatures of the forest, from colorful birds to the forest animals, gathered to join this festival. Birds with rainbow feathers, flowers of many colors, and even little insects with bright wings took part.

At the start of the festival, the colorful birds flew up into the sky and circled the small festival fires that had been lit on the ground. Flowers of many colors danced and enjoyed their own songs, and insects with bright wings danced beside the little flames.

At this festival, every creature of the forest danced and played, and everyone together made a happy, unforgettable day. This festival was a special day full of bright, living colors, and all the creatures of nature enjoyed it and kept it in their hearts.

And that was the story of the Festival of Colors, a celebration held in the forest every year, filling everyone with the colors of joy.

The End.
