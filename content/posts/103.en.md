In a zoo far from the city, where there were tall trees covered with green leaves and colorful flowers, there lived a big brown bear named Brownie. Brownie Bear was the most popular of all the animals in the zoo because he was good to all his friends and always jumped around with a smile on his face.

One day, a group of children from the city came to the zoo. Together with their trainer, they looked around with wonder and asked questions of different animals. When they reached Brownie Bear, he greeted them with a warm smile.

Brownie Bear told children beautiful stories about life in the forest. He talked about the days he used to play in the forest with his other bear friends, and about the herd of big brown bears that used to feed and play together.

"Brownie Bear, why are you so happy?" asked one of the children.

Brownie Bear said with a big smile, "I always treat my friends well and make friends with everyone. Being friendly with others and respecting all animals is the most important thing I learned at the zoo."

"We also like to be friends and always respect everyone," said one of the children.

Brownie Bear gave a small laugh and told them, "Always respect everyone and be friends with others, taking care of the animals and nature around us is one of the most important things we can learn."

The children said goodbye to the zoo with a smile and returned home with a promise to come here again. They were happy with Brownie Bear's stories and the lessons they learned from him and were looking forward to seeing the lovely zoo animals again.

In this way, Brownie Bear and the children of the city, learning from each other, always remembered that friendship and respect for all animals and the environment is one of the most important things to care about.

The End.
