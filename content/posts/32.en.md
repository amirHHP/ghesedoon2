In a green, lively forest lived a little fairy named Luna. Luna was a fairy with shining wings and eyes like the stars of the night. She always had a smile on her lips and loved to help others and give them joy.

One morning, while Luna was sitting on a big flower, she heard a sound from far away. It was the cry of a little rabbit. Luna flew quickly toward the sound and found the little rabbit caught among thorny bushes. Luna said kindly, “Don’t worry. I’m here to help you!”

With her magic wings she pushed the thorny bushes aside and set the little rabbit free. The rabbit looked at Luna with grateful eyes and said, “Thank you so much, Luna! You saved me!”

Luna smiled and said, “It was what I had to do. Whenever you need help, just call me.”

The little rabbit went back to his friends with joy, and Luna went on flying through the forest. Wherever she went, she stroked the flowers and made the birds happy. Wherever she went, she brought joy and light.

Another day, while Luna was resting beside a big tree, she heard another sound. This time it was the whimper of a kitten. Luna flew quickly toward the sound and found a kitten stuck in the branches of a tree, unable to come down.

Luna said to the kitten, “Don’t be afraid. I’m here to help you!” She flew gently to the top of the tree, lifted the kitten with her wings, and brought him back to the ground. The kitten said happily, “Thank you, Luna! I was so scared.”

Luna stroked the kitten and said, “Always remember that your friends will always help you. I am always here.”

The kitten ran toward home with joy, and Luna went on flying through the forest again. She loved seeing other people’s smiles and happiness, and these deeds made her very glad.

Another day, Luna flew toward a clear spring to drink a little water. When she reached the spring, she saw a little baby bird who had fallen in the water and could not get out. Luna hurried close to the baby bird and carefully lifted him from the water.

The baby bird looked at Luna with fear and said, “Thank you so much, Luna! I didn’t know how to get out of the water.”

Luna smiled and said, “All of us need help sometimes. I’m glad I could help you.”

The baby bird said goodbye to Luna with wet feathers but a warm heart, and Luna went on flying through the forest.

With every day that passed, Luna helped more and more new friends and made more hearts glad. She learned that with love and kindness, the world can be made more beautiful. Our little fairy, with a heart full of love and friendship, was always ready to help everyone who needed it.

And so the kind little fairy named Luna lived in the beautiful, lively forest, and every day, with new adventures and new friends, she made a world full of light and joy.

The End.
