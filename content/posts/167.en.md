In a small, delightful town, the toys lived in the children’s homes. Every toy, with different colors and personalities, had good friends and always played together. But one day, they decided to hold a contest to choose the best toy.

Papi Pupi, a soft yellow doll, was the leader of this contest. With her big eyes, she invited everyone to the contest. Papi Pupi was always full of energy and joy, and today too, with her cheerful mood, she made everyone glad.

When the start of the contest was announced, all the toys gathered together. Godzilla, a big silver dinosaur with teeth drawn on his clothes, was one of the other players. With spinning, brave movements, he amazed everyone.

After Godzilla, it was Pony Pixie’s turn. She was a little rainbow toy who, with her colorful hair and beautiful movements, drew everyone’s heart to her. Pony Pixie, with her colorful clothes and the wings designed on her back, amazed everyone.

After these, it was the turn of all the other toys. Each one took part in the contest in the very best way, with their own special qualities and personalities. Fisher Fox, with his curved eyes and blue clothes, showed how well he could swim and catch things from the sea. Golden Bear, with his yellow coat and long tail, showed his happy, calming movements.

At the end of the contest, all the toys came together to the funny decision of the judges. Papi Pupi leaned lazily on Silver Armanda’s arms, and everyone enjoyed this funny moment. But it seemed that all the toys were winners, because each of them had shown themselves in the very best way in the contest.

After the contest, all the toys went back to their homes with happiness. With happy words and laughter, they kept this day as a beautiful memory in their minds. This contest showed that every toy, with its own special personality and qualities, can have good friends, and that with play and contests they can always be happy and glad.

This story reminds children that with friendship and play, they can turn every day into a happy day to remember, and that they can have beautiful, joyful memories of all their toys.

The End.
