Once upon a time, in a far land, there lived a king named Nima in a city called Greental, a place where everyone had peace and happiness. Nima was a king who, with a heart full of kindness and courage, always looked for ways to bring joy and happiness to his people.

Nima's castle stood on the top of a mountain, and from there he could look out over the whole city and the land around it. Every day, Nima rode around the city with his loyal horse and talked with his people. He treated every citizen, from the smallest child to the old men and women, with respect and friendship, and he always cared about them.

One day, Nima went with his loyal friend, White Wolf, to a garden of fragrant, beautiful flowers and fruit. This garden was at the edge of the city, and it never withered or grew wild. Nima and White Wolf walked around the garden and talked warmly with every citizen who was there, and asked how they could make the garden better and more beautiful. All the people joined in this shared work with eagerness and high energy.

In the same way, Nima took pride and care in every task that would make his citizens' lives better. Every day, by working to improve the city and its happiness, he was an example of leadership and kindness that had a good effect on all his people. With will and energy, he always looked for ways to make his people's lives better, and he led everyone toward happiness and joy.

Nima and White Wolf always stood together, and with love and friendship they served his citizens. Every day they brought joy and peace to the hearts of all the people of Greental. Even though he was a little king, with his effort and spirit his city became a world of peace, calm, and joy, where all the people enjoyed their lives and helped one another make their city better and more beautiful every day.

The End.
