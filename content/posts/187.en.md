On a warm, sunny day, in a small village, life was always happy and full of the color and scent of tasty fruit. In this village lived a kind woman named Mrs. Golya. She had a little garden full of delicious fruit like apples, oranges, peaches, and grapes.

Golya always woke up early in the morning and went to her garden to pick her fruit. With her big, kind hands she took the fruit from the trees and gathered it in a bright fruit basket. Her basket was truly a work of art, filled with all the colors of life.

One day, while Golya was picking fruit, a little child named Milad joined her. Milad was a small, energetic boy who always loved to go to gardens and the world of nature and learn new things.

Golya welcomed Milad to her garden with a warm smile. She showed him how to pick the fruit and wash the apples under the green leaves. Milad tried with all his excitement to do well everything Mrs. Golya taught him.

After the basket of tasty fruit was full, Golya and Milad went back to the house. They set the basket in the kitchen and went on with their day. Every day Golya picked tasty fruit from her garden and put it in the basket, and every time Milad was with her, enjoying the colors and the smell of the fruit.

Golya’s basket of tasty fruit always reminded everyone of a world of happy, energetic life in the village. Everyone enjoyed this tasty fruit and the fresh scent that came from Golya’s garden, and they always remembered with a smile how Golya and Milad had found all this beauty together.

The End.
