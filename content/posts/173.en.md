In a faraway forest full of beauty, there lived a kind donkey named Lulu. Lulu was a good-hearted, kind donkey who always ran through the forest with a smile on his lips and was friends with everyone. His gentleness and kindness made all the animals in the forest respect him and be his friends.

Lulu lived in the forest with his friends. One of his close friends was a clever fox named Rudy. Rudy was a fox with red eyes and a fluffy tail who, with his beautiful movements and clever tricks, could always solve any problem. Lulu always went looking for adventure with Rudy, and together they discovered new things in the forest.

Another of Lulu’s friends was a fast-footed rabbit named Benji. Benji was a rabbit with long ears and lots of energy who, with his speed and hops, could always get away from trouble. Lulu was always friends with Benji and always helped him get out of the troubles he faced.

In the forest, all the animals enjoyed Lulu’s kindness and friendship. He was friends with the big birds who sang in the branches of the trees, with the big, pleasant bears who slept in the shade under the trees, and even with the little lizards who lived under the soil.

One day, Lulu and his friends went on a trip through the forest. They came to a tall tree full of tasty fruits. Together they enjoyed nature and the fresh, tasty fruits, and Lulu, with a smile on his lips, said to his friends, “I am glad that I have friends like you, who always go looking for the joys of life together.”

Lulu’s friends all smiled at him with eyes full of joy and said, “We always love to be with you too, dear Lulu.”

This story reminds children that with a heart full of kindness and cheerfulness, they can always make friends and, together on the path of life, feel happiness and joy—just like the kind donkey Lulu and his forest friends.

The End.
