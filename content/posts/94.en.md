On a calm, beautiful day, a little girl named Leila was playing in the garden of her house. Leila loved the stars and space, and every night she looked at the stars and the moon in the sky. She always wished for a day when she could go to the moon and see its big, bright craters up close.

One day, after hearing stories of the moon from her mother and father, Leila decided to travel to the moon. She got ready with a magic boat that had been given to her. The magic boat was made of oak wood and was full of shining, rainbow lights that appeared in the dark of night.

With the help of her lucky friend, a talking cat named Kitty, Leila traveled to the moon. Kitty gave Leila important directions. "When we go toward the moon, we must pass right through the galaxy to get there."

Leila and Kitty found their way together. On their path they met a few new friends, like stars and birds with rainbow feathers. Everyone they saw welcomed them with smiles and joy.

As time passed, the magic boat moved toward the moon. Leila and Kitty were amazed by the beauties of space and the lovely views they saw from the boat's window. Soon they drew near the moon, and as they arrived they were happy to see even more friends who joined them from other parts of space.

After arriving on the moon, Leila and Kitty met a gathering of friendly, kind space creatures. They told Leila how she could fly over the surface of the moon and see the big craters up close.

With the help of her friends on the moon, Leila spent nights with the great moon and the bright craters they looked at together. She always remembered that with friendship and courage she could overcome any challenge and go anywhere her heart wished.

When it was time to go home, Leila and Kitty said goodbye with a big, happy celebration. Their space friends gave her a magic gift to remember: a moon doll with its big craters.

Going home was easy for Leila and Kitty. They were always happy about their journey to the moon and the friendships they had made there. This journey to the moon was one of the best adventures of Leila's life, and she would never forget it.

The End.
