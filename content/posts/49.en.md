Once upon a time, in a small, beautiful village, there lived a girl named Leila. Leila loved colors and magical stories. Every day she sat watching the sky and waited for a beautiful rainbow to appear. She had always heard from her grandmother that at the end of the rainbow a treasure is hidden.

One day, after a summer rain, Leila noticed a big, beautiful rainbow painted in the sky. She shouted with excitement, “Grandma, look! A rainbow!”

Grandma smiled and said, “Yes, my dear. Now it is time for you to go toward the end of the rainbow and see if you can find the treasure.”

Leila said with eyes full of eagerness, “Really? May I go?”

Grandma nodded and said, “Yes, but remember to always be kind and brave.”

Leila set off with a little backpack and a bottle of water. She followed the path of the rainbow and went on her way. On the road she met a little white rabbit named Nani who seemed lost. Leila said kindly, “Hello, Nani. Why are you so unhappy?”

Nani said unhappily, “I have lost my home, and I don’t know how to go back.”

Leila smiled and said, “Don’t worry. I am going toward the end of the rainbow. Come with me. Maybe on the way we will find your home.”

Nani accepted happily, and they went on together. A little farther on, they came to a river. Leila and Nani were looking for a way to cross the river when suddenly a big turtle named Tani came close and said, “Hello, little friends! Do you need help?”

Leila said politely, “Hello, Tani! Yes, we want to cross this river, but we don’t know how.”

Tani smiled and said, “I can take you to the other side of the river. Climb onto my back.”

Leila and Nani climbed onto Tani’s back with joy, and he carried them gently to the other side of the river. Leila thanked Tani, and they continued on their way.

After a while, they reached a forest full of tall trees and colorful flowers. Leila and Nani looked around in wonder. In the middle of the forest they saw a little hedgehog named Rizi who seemed to be searching for something. Leila asked, “Hello, Rizi. Are you looking for something?”

Rizi said with worry, “Hello, yes! I have lost my magic totem, and without it I cannot go home.”

Leila said with confidence, “We are going toward the end of the rainbow. Come with us. Maybe on the way we will find your totem.”

Rizi joined them too, and now Leila, Nani, and Rizi went on together. At the end of the forest, the rainbow suddenly grew closer and brighter. Leila said with eagerness, “Look! We are near the end of the rainbow!”

When they reached the end of the rainbow, a big, beautiful shine welcomed them. In the middle of the shine they found a treasure full of colorful jewels. Leila said happily, “Oh, this is the treasure Grandma told me about!”

But more than the treasure, Leila was glad that on this journey she had found new friends and helped them. Nani reached her home, Rizi found her magic totem, and they all went back to the village together.

Grandma smiled and said, “Well done, Leila! You showed that with kindness and courage, you can reach any treasure.”

Leila said with pride, “Grandma, the best treasure is the good friends I found on this journey.”

And from that day on, Leila always remembered that with kindness and courage, she can solve any problem and find any treasure.

The End.
