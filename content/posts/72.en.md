Once upon a time, in a small, beautiful village, there lived a little girl named Nazanin. Nazanin was always glad and happy, and she loved to make everyone happy. She had many friends, and all the people of the village loved her.

One day, while Nazanin was playing in the forest, she suddenly found a small, beautiful box hidden under a tree. The box looked very old and was decorated with shiny, sparkling jewels. Nazanin picked up the box with excitement and took it home.

When Nazanin reached home, she decided to open the box and see what was inside. She opened the lid carefully and saw, to her surprise, a small, beautiful lamp. The lamp was shiny and bright, and Nazanin understood at once that this was a magic lamp.

Nazanin picked up the lamp happily and showed it to everyone. She said to her friends, her mother and father, and all the people of the village, “Look! I have found a magic lamp!” Everyone looked at the lamp with surprise and wonder and enjoyed its beauty.

Nazanin decided to light the magic lamp. She lit it carefully, and suddenly a bright, beautiful light came out of the lamp. The light of the lamp reached every place in the village and made everything bright and shiny.

But this magic lamp did not only give light. Anyone who looked at the lamp’s light suddenly felt happiness and joy. The children began to laugh and play, the grown-ups talked together and smiled, and the whole village filled with joy and happiness.

Nazanin was so glad to see these scenes, and she understood that this magic lamp truly was a gift that makes everyone happy. She decided to light this magic lamp every day so that all the people of the village would always be happy.

The days passed, and every day Nazanin lit the magic lamp, and all the people of the village enjoyed the light and joy of the lamp. They understood that with happiness and friendship they could have a better life and solve their problems more easily.

One day, while Nazanin was playing in the forest, she suddenly heard a faint sound. She followed the sound and saw a small, beautiful bird hurt under a tree. Nazanin picked up the bird and took it home. With care and kindness she cared for the bird and gave it food and water.

After a few days, the little bird felt better and could fly again. The bird thanked Nazanin happily and said, “You are so kind and lovable. I want to give you a special gift.” Then the bird gave Nazanin a small, shining seed.

The bird said, “This seed is magic. If you plant it, a beautiful, big tree will grow that can grant your wishes.” Nazanin took the seed happily and planted it in her garden.

The days passed, and the little tree began to grow. Every day Nazanin watered the tree and cared for it. The tree grew bigger and bigger, and its branches filled with beautiful, colorful flowers. Nazanin understood that this magic tree could grant her wishes.

Nazanin wished that all the people of the village would always be happy and well and have no troubles. The magic tree granted her wish, and all the people of the village stayed happy and glad always.

From then on, Nazanin visited the magic tree every day and enjoyed its beauty and magic. She understood that with kindness and love you can make the world a better place, and she always tried to help others and make them happy.

And that is how Nazanin and the people of the village always lived together with happiness and friendship, and every day was full of light and joy.

The End.
