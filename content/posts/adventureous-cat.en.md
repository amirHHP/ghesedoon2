Once upon a time, in a small, beautiful village, there lived a cat named Pishi. Pishi was a small white cat with bright green eyes who loved adventure. He always wanted to discover new places and find new friends.

One beautiful, sunny day, Pishi decided to go to the forest near the village and explore it. He told his mother, “Mama, I want to go to the forest and see what interesting things are there.”

Pishi’s mother smiled and said, “All right, my dear, but be careful and don’t be late. The forest is a big place, and you might get lost.”

Pishi said happily, “Don’t worry, Mama. I am very smart, and I will come back soon.”

Pishi ran toward the forest and enjoyed the beauties around him. Tall green trees, colorful flowers, and sweet-voiced birds were everywhere. Pishi watched everything carefully and enjoyed the smell of the flowers and the sound of the birds.

The farther Pishi went into the forest, the more he was drawn to its beauties. He followed butterflies, jumped over stones, and played with playful squirrels. But suddenly he noticed that he had gone very far from home.

Pishi felt a little afraid and tried to find the way back, but no matter how much he searched, he could not find the right path. He said in his heart, “Oh no, I am lost! What should I do now?”

Pishi began to call, “Mama! Mama!” But no sound came from his mother. He grew more afraid and thought, “I must find a way to get home.”

Just then, Pishi saw a small white rabbit named Bunny who was chewing fresh leaves. Pishi went up to Bunny with worry and said, “Hello, Bunny. I am Pishi, and I am lost. Can you help me find the way home?”

Bunny smiled and said, “Hello, Pishi! Don’t worry. I can help you. Come, let’s find the way to your house together.”

Bunny led Pishi with his quick hops. They passed beside rivers, under big trees, and over wooden bridges. Bunny showed different paths, and Pishi followed him carefully.

On the way they came to a wise owl named Owly. Owly was sitting on a tall branch and looking down carefully. Bunny said to Owly, “Hello, Owly! This is Pishi, and he is lost. Can you help us find the way to his house?”

Owly said in a calm, wise voice, “Hello, Pishi and Bunny! Yes, I can help you. Pishi, you must go this way until you reach a big tree with a bent branch. From there you can find the way home.”

Pishi thanked Owly happily and went toward the big tree with Bunny. When they reached the tree, Pishi saw the bent branch and remembered that this was the same path he had come from.

Pishi said happily, “Oh, yes! I remember now. I came this way. Thank you so much, dear Bunny and Owly.”

Bunny and Owly smiled and said, “We are glad we could help you. Take care of yourself, and don’t come to the forest alone again.”

Pishi ran quickly toward his house, and when he arrived, his mother was waiting for him in the yard with worry. Pishi jumped happily into his mother’s arms and said, “Mama, I came back! Thank you for always teaching me to be brave and to find good friends.”

Pishi’s mother smiled and said, “I am glad you came back safe, my dear. Always remember that wherever you go, be careful and take care.”

And from that day on, Pishi always went on new adventures carefully and with his friends, and he enjoyed every moment of his life.

The End.
