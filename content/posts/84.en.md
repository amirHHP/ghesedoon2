In a world where anything was possible, there lived the Sun and the Moon. The Sun, with his light and warmth, made everyone happy and full of energy. He began the day with his shine and stayed until sunset with the children who were playing. The Moon was the Sun's good friend, with a soft, kind light that shone on the earth at night.

One time, the Sun and the Moon decided to travel to a new world. They began their journey and crossed fields and mountains and rivers. On this journey they found many new friends, and their light and their care for their friends brought joy to everyone.

One day, the Sun and the Moon went to see a little girl named Sara. Sara was a happy, energetic girl who always looked at the world with a smile. She loved the Sun and the Moon, and every night before sleep she said hello to them.

The Sun smiled at Sara and said, "Hello, dear Sara. How are you tonight?"

Sara answered happily, "Hello, Sun. I am very well. How are you and the Moon?"

The Moon answered Sara gently, "We are always well, and we are glad that you are beside us too."

Sara said with a smile, "I always look at you and listen to your stories. You know, dear Sun, whenever I look at you from behind the clouds, I feel I want to dance just like you."

The Sun and the Moon were happy about Sara's stories and promised her that every night they would tell her a new story from their own world. From that day on, every night before sleep, Sara listened to happy, lovely stories with her friends the Sun and the Moon, in a world full of light and care for friends.

The End.
