Once upon a time, in a green and beautiful forest, many animals lived. Among them was a small, pretty butterfly named Nazanin. Nazanin had colorful, shining wings, and all the animals loved her. Every day she flew happily through the forest and sipped from the beautiful flowers.

One day, Nazanin felt very tired, and her wings could no longer fly as they used to. She sat on a big, soft flower and told her friends, "I am so tired. I feel I need to rest."

Nazanin's friends grew worried and said, "Nazanin, maybe you should go home and rest a little." Nazanin agreed and went home to rest.

Days passed, and Nazanin stayed at home resting. Her friends came to see her every day and hoped she would get better and play with them again. But Nazanin was still tired and could not fly.

One day, Nazanin felt a strange change in her body. She told her friends, "I feel that something is changing. I do not know what this feeling is." Her friends grew worried and said, "Nazanin, we are always beside you and will take care of you."

Little by little, Nazanin went into a soft, warm cocoon. Inside the cocoon she grew calm and felt that the time for rest and change had come. Days and nights passed, and Nazanin fell into a deep sleep inside the cocoon.

After a while, a sunny, beautiful day arrived. The cocoon slowly split open, and Nazanin came out. With surprise and joy she saw that her wings were strong and beautiful again. She felt as if she had been born again and was ready to fly once more.

Nazanin flew into the sky with her new wings and joined her friends, who were waiting for her. All the animals of the forest were happy to see Nazanin again and cried out with joy, "Nazanin has come back to us! She has been born again!"

Nazanin played with her friends and enjoyed the beauties of the forest. She told all the animals, "I am so happy that I can be with you and fly again. This new birth showed me that we should always have hope and patience and not be afraid of the changes in life."

The forest animals learned from Nazanin's words and understood that changes in life are not always bad and can help us grow stronger and better. They lived beside Nazanin with joy and kindness, and every day was full of friendship and happiness.

And that is how Nazanin, the small, pretty butterfly, showed everyone with her new birth that hope and patience can lead us to better things. The green, beautiful forest was full of joy and friendship, and all the animals lived together and enjoyed every moment.

The End.
