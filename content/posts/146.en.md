Once upon a time, in a forest full of color and beauty, there lived many birds. These different birds traveled from place to place to find food and a good life. One of them was a bird named Peter. Peter was a migrating bird who, every year when the cold season came, traveled with his family to warmer places.

Peter had shining feathers and a strong way of flying. He always asked about the birds' migration paths and looked for useful news for his family. One of Peter's friends was a bird named Leila. Leila had always asked Peter to come with her on the migration path.

One day, Peter and Leila set out together on a long migration journey. They looked for other birds who were migrating too. Along with other bird families, like the sparrows and the crows, they joined a path full of adventure.

When they reached the longest part of the path, the sky filled with a rainbow. This was a sign for Peter and Leila that they were heading toward warm lands. They came to a freshwater lake where the birds stayed to rest.

The lake was full of many kinds of fish. Peter and Leila loved watching the fish move under the water. They were full of energy and enjoyed the trip and seeing the world.

After a helpful rest, Peter and Leila went on with another group of birds. They always enjoyed the beautiful paths and the wonderful views together, and they thought of their lovely memories.

At last, Peter and Leila, along with the other families, came to peace in a green, beautiful meadow. They were always good friends, and they enjoyed seeing one another and traveling together on the migration path. This shows how important and precious it is to stay together and help one another, even on long journeys.

The End.
