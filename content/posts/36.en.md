In a far, beautiful land lived a kind king named King Nader. King Nader loved his garden and spent most of his time there. The king’s garden was full of colorful flowers, fruit trees, and clear springs. Every day he walked in his garden with a smile on his lips and enjoyed its beauty.

King Nader always told his helpers, “My garden is like a treasure. We must take good care of it.”

One morning, while King Nader was walking in the garden, he noticed that one of his fruit trees had become sick. The tree’s leaves had turned yellow, and its fruit was slowly drying. King Nader came close to the tree with worry and looked at it carefully.

He said to himself, “This tree needs help. I must find out how I can save it.”

King Nader decided to ask the Wise One of the land, who always had good answers. He went toward the Wise One’s castle and found him. When the Wise One heard the king’s trouble, he smiled and said, “Don’t worry, kind king. I will tell you a secret that will help your garden.”

The Wise One told the king that he must talk with the trees and show them love. He explained that trees, like people, need love and care. King Nader listened with surprise and eagerness and decided to try this answer.

The next day, King Nader went back to the garden and walked toward the sick tree. He said gently, “Hello, my dear friend. I am King Nader, and I care about you very much. Don’t worry. I am here to help you.”

Every day the king talked with the tree and showed it love. He also told his helpers to pay attention to the other trees and talk with them too. As time passed, the sick tree’s leaves slowly turned green again, and its fruit became fresh and tasty once more.

King Nader was very happy that the trees of his garden were lively and beautiful again. He learned how important love and care are, and how they can help plants and even people.

Another day, while King Nader was walking in the garden, he noticed a little wounded bird lying on the ground. He gently picked up the bird and took it to the palace. The king treated the bird with care and kindness and gave it food. After a few days the bird got well and flew into the sky with joy.

Seeing the bird able to fly again, King Nader smiled and said, “Love and care can work wonders. Even the smallest creatures need looking after.”

Little by little, King Nader’s garden became a place full of wonder and beauty. Different animals came to the garden, and the king became friends with all of them. He treated everyone with gentleness and love and cared for his garden in the best way he could.

One day the people of the village told King Nader, “Kind king, your garden is the best and most beautiful garden we have ever seen. We have learned from you how to treat nature and animals with love and care.”

King Nader said with pride and happiness, “This garden is the fruit of love and care. All of us can make a better world with love and kindness.”

From then on, King Nader showed love and care not only to his garden, but to all the people of his land. He taught them how to live with love and kindness and to look after nature and living creatures. And so King Nader’s land became a place full of peace and joy.

The End.
