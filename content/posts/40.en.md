In a small, faraway town there was a little library that all the children loved to visit and read different books. In this library there was a special magic book called the Enchanted Book. Because of its magic power, this book could show children the future.

One of the children who loved to read very much was a girl named Melissa. She always visited the town library and was always eager and excited to read new books. One day, while Melissa was in the library, her eye fell on the Enchanted Book. When she saw the shining, beautiful book, she was drawn to it and decided to read it.

Melissa opened the book carefully and looked at it page by page. Suddenly the book began to shine, and a kind, quiet voice rose from it: “Hello, Melissa! I am the Enchanted Book. I can show you a future from your life.”

Melissa said in surprise, “Hello, Enchanted Book! Can you really show the future?”

The Enchanted Book said with a smile, “Yes, I can. But you must remember that the future always depends on your decisions and choices. I can only show a picture of your future.”

Melissa said excitedly, “I would like to see what my future will be like. Can you show me?”

The Enchanted Book opened a page and showed Melissa a picture of a bright, beautiful future. She saw herself in a big garden with colorful flowers and fruit trees, playing with her friends. Everyone was laughing with joy and happiness, and the sky was blue and clear.

Melissa said in wonder, “This is so beautiful! I would like to always be happy with my friends.”

The Enchanted Book said with a smile, “Yes. This is a future you can reach with love and kindness. Whenever you get lost on the path of your life, remember that I am always here to help you.”

Melissa said with tears of joy in her eyes, “Thank you, Enchanted Book! I will always remember to build a better future with kindness and helping others.”

From then on, Melissa always kept reading and studying with the help of the Enchanted Book. In her life she always tried to help others and treated the people around her with kindness. She talked with her friends about the power of the Enchanted Book, and everyone learned that with love and care they can create a bright, good future for themselves and others.

In this way, Melissa, with the Enchanted Book and a heart full of kindness, was always looking for a better future and appeared every day with hope and joy.

The End.
