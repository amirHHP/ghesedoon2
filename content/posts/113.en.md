Once upon a time, in a faraway land, there was a castle known as the Crystal Castle. This large, beautiful castle was made of clear, shining crystals, and when the sunlight fell on it, it sparkled like a brilliant jewel. In this castle lived a small, kind prince named Luna.

Luna loved the Crystal Castle and the beautiful gardens around it. Every morning he played in the castle garden with colorful birds and pretty butterflies. One morning, while Luna was playing, he heard a cry in the distance. He went toward the sound and saw a small, sad rabbit sitting under a bush.

Luna asked kindly, "What is wrong, little rabbit? Why are you crying?"

The rabbit cried, "I am lost, and I do not know how to get home."

Luna smiled and said, "Do not be afraid. I will help you. Let's go back to the castle together and ask the king and queen for help."

The rabbit happily followed Luna to the Crystal Castle. When they arrived, Luna told the king and queen the rabbit's story. The king said kindly, "Do not worry. We will help the little rabbit get home."

The queen said, "But first, let us welcome the rabbit. He must be hungry and tired."

The rabbit happily thanked the king and queen for their kindness, and they went with Luna to the great hall of the castle. The hall was full of light and beautiful colors shining from the castle crystals. They sat together and enjoyed the tasty food the servants had brought.

After the meal, Luna and the rabbit decided to go to the castle garden and play some more. They played in the garden, smelled the flowers, and danced with the butterflies. The rabbit said, "This castle and its garden are so beautiful. I have never seen a place like this."

Luna smiled and said, "I am so glad you are here too. Now let's go ask the king and queen to take you home."

They went back to the castle, and the king and queen told the servants to take the rabbit home. Luna said, "I want to go with the rabbit to his house and say goodbye."

The king and queen happily agreed, and Luna and the rabbit, with the servants, set off for the rabbit's house. They walked through the beautiful woods around the castle and at last reached the rabbit's home.

The rabbit said happily, "This is my house. Thank you so much for helping me."

Luna said kindly, "I am glad we could help you. Whenever you like, you can come to the Crystal Castle and play with me."

The rabbit smiled and said, "I surely will. You are my best friend."

Luna and the rabbit said goodbye, and Luna returned to the castle with the servants. He was very happy that he had been able to help the rabbit and make a new friend.

From that day on, the rabbit sometimes came to the Crystal Castle and played with Luna. They ran in the beautiful gardens, enjoyed the flowers, and danced with the butterflies. With new friendships and happy adventures, the Crystal Castle was full of life and joy.

Luna understood that helping others and being friends with them was the best gift he could receive. Every day he went to the garden with a smile and waited for new friends, so they could have new adventures together. And the Crystal Castle, with its shine and beauty, remained a place of happiness and friendship.

The End.
