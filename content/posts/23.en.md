In a far land, where the sky was always full of shining stars, a magic night was about to happen. All the stars had gathered in the sky and were waiting to celebrate the birth of a new star. This little star had not yet come into the world, but everyone was talking about her and waiting to see her.

Nearby, on the earth, there lived a little boy named Soroush. Soroush loved watching the night sky and the shining stars. Every night, before he went to sleep, he looked at the sky and talked with the stars. He always wished that he could be one of the stars of the sky and shine beside them.

One night, while Soroush was watching the sky, he suddenly saw a shining light coming closer from far away. He looked at that light with surprise and excitement. The light came closer and closer, until at last he understood that this shining light was the new star who was about to be born.

Soroush said to himself, "How beautiful she is! This star must be very special and important."

At that same moment, the older stars welcomed the new star and gave her a place in the sky. The new star was born with the name Setareh, and she slowly began to shine. She was still small, but with a heart full of light and hope, she wanted to show everyone how beautiful and bright she was.

Setareh said in a soft, quiet voice, "Hello, friends! I am Setareh. I am so happy to have joined you."

The other stars answered with smiles, "Hello, Setareh! We are so happy that you are here too. Let us show you the adventures of the sky."

From the earth, Soroush watched this scene with a smile and wished that he could talk with the stars. Suddenly something strange happened. One of the big, old stars, named Mahrokh, sent out a light from herself and invited Soroush toward the sky.

Soroush looked at the light with big, surprised eyes and felt that he was being lifted toward the sky. He flew slowly toward the stars, and when he reached them, the stars welcomed him happily.

Mahrokh said, "Soroush, we know that you love the stars. Today we have invited you to join the birthday of the new star and see up close how beautiful she is."

Soroush said happily, "Thank you so much! This was my greatest wish, and it has come true."

The new star looked at Soroush with a smile and said, "Hello, Soroush! I am Setareh. I am happy that you have come to this birthday feast too."

Soroush and Setareh became friends, and from that moment on they played together in the sky every night. Setareh showed Soroush how he could shine and give his light to the whole world. Soroush, with a heart full of love and hope, helped Setareh become brighter every day.

The other stars joined them too, and all together they shone in the night sky and told one another beautiful, kind stories. They understood that with friendship and love they can make the world more beautiful and show everyone that light and hope always shine in their hearts.

And that is how Soroush and Setareh, with their friendship and love, filled the nights of the sky with light and joy. Every night, when Soroush returned to the earth and went to sleep, he knew that his new friend, Setareh, was awake in the sky and sending him light.

This story shows that with kind friends and a heart full of light, we can turn the world into a better place. Setareh and Soroush were always together and never forgot how important it is to give light and love to others.

The End.
