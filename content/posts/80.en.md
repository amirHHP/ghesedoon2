In a small village called Freshbloom, a nut-seller had a little shop where he sold magic sweets. The nut-seller's name was Mr. Ghahraman. He made sweets that had magic powers.

One of Mr. Ghahraman's most loved sweets were the colorful sweets. Each of these sweets had a special gift. The yellow sweet made anyone who ate it fresh and full of energy. The green sweet made anyone who ate it remember good thoughts. And the blue sweet made anyone who ate it feel calm and peaceful.

One day, a little girl named Leila came to Mr. Ghahraman's shop with her mother. Leila loved the magic sweets. When they stepped inside, Leila's little eyes fell on the colorful sweets. She said to Mr. Ghahraman, "Mr. Ghahraman, we want a magic sweet!"

Mr. Ghahraman smiled and said to Leila and her mother, "Well then, look at my magic sweets. Today I have made special sweets just for you."

Leila came in excitedly and looked at the colorful sweets with care. She decided to eat the yellow sweet so she would feel fresh and full of energy. Leila's mother chose the green sweet so she would remember good thoughts.

After eating the magic sweets, Leila and her mother felt wonderful. They left the shop with smiles and went back to the village of Freshbloom. From then on, whenever Leila or her mother needed joy or peace, they went to Mr. Ghahraman's shop and ate one of his magic sweets.

And so, thanks to Mr. Ghahraman's magic sweets, Leila and her mother were always happy and lively, and they always remembered good thoughts, especially when they sat together and enjoyed these special sweets.

The End.
