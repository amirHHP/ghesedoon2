Once upon a time, in a small village by the sea, there lived a little boy named Arman. Arman loved the sea and played for hours on the shore every day, listening to the sound of the waves. He always wished to see a real mermaid.

One day, while Arman was building a sandcastle, he heard a soft voice from the sea calling him. Arman lifted his head and looked at the sea. There, near the shore, a beautiful mermaid with golden hair and blue eyes was smiling at him. Arman said in wonder, “Oh! Are you a mermaid?”

The mermaid said with a sweet smile, “Yes, my name is Marjan. I want to be your friend. Would you like to be my friend too?”

Arman said excitedly, “Yes, of course! This is wonderful!”

From that day on, Arman and Marjan played together on the shore every day. Marjan told Arman beautiful stories from the world under the sea, and Arman told Marjan stories of life on land. They were the best of friends and enjoyed spending time together very much.

One day, Marjan said to Arman, “Arman, would you like to come to the world under the sea and have an adventure with me?”

Arman said eagerly, “Oh, yes! I would like that very much.”

Marjan gave Arman a magic necklace and said, “This necklace lets you breathe under the water and come with me to the world under the sea.”

Arman put the necklace around his neck and went into the water with Marjan. He looked around in wonder and saw that the world under the sea was full of colors and wonderful creatures. Colorful fish, beautiful shells, and tall sea plants were all around them.

Marjan took Arman to see a big, beautiful palace where she lived with her family. Arman looked at the palace in wonder and said, “This palace is so beautiful, Marjan!”

Marjan smiled and said, “Thank you, Arman. Now let’s go see my friends together.”

They went to see Marjan’s friends, the dolphins, the turtles, and the colorful fish. Everyone was happy to see Arman and invited him to joyful, exciting games. Arman had never been so happy and excited.

When the day ended, Marjan brought Arman back to the surface and said, “Arman, I am so glad I became your friend. Whenever you wish, you can come to the world under the sea and play with me.”

Arman smiled and said, “Thank you, Marjan. I am so glad to have you as a friend too. I promise I will always come to see you.”

From that day on, Arman and Marjan met on the shore every day and continued their wonderful adventures. They learned that friendship and love can break any wall and even make a bond between the world of land and the sea.

Arman went home with a heart full of joy and beautiful memories, and every night before sleep he thought of the world under the sea and his dear friend, Marjan. He knew that nothing in the world is more important than having good, kind friends.

And so it was that Arman and Marjan, a little boy from the village by the sea and a beautiful mermaid from the world under the water, became the best of friends, and the story of their friendship stayed forever in the heart of the sea and the land.

The End.
