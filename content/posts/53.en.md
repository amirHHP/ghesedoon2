Once upon a time, three little friends named Milo, Susan, and Leila decided to go on a lovely, happy adventure in the mountains. They had always wanted to discover a new world and go to places they had never seen. They played together, they laughed together, and they were the very best of friends.

One early morning, when the sun was still painting everything gold, Milo smiled a brand-new smile and said to his friends, “Let’s go to the mountains! Big adventures are waiting for us there.”

Susan said with a sparkle, “Yes, yes! I want to see the tall Alps and the wild mountain flowers!”

Leila said with a giggle, “And I want to see the blue mountain sky and watch the birds fly!”

The friends decided to go. They put on the right clothes, packed food and water, and set off toward the mountains with joy.

When they reached the foot of the mountains, Milo said bravely, “It is very high, but we can do it!”

Susan and Leila followed Milo’s steps with eager hearts. They walked under shady trees and between big rocks, and they climbed toward the high mountain peaks.

When they reached the highest point, they opened their eyes to all the beauty of the mountains. Far away, the sky was calm and bright and blue, and the ground was covered with wild flowers and green grass.

Susan said with excitement, “How beautiful it is! Everything here shines in so many colors.”

Leila said with a giggle, “Really! I feel as if I am walking on a brand-new land.”

The three friends stood side by side and spent a little while with one another and with the beautiful wild world. They played and hopped from rock to rock, and they filled the moment with joy and life.

After a short time, they decided to go back down the mountain. On the way home, Susan found a lovely mountain flower full of bright colors.

Susan said eagerly to her friends, “Look! What a beautiful flower I found!”

Leila said with a giggle, “It really is beautiful! Let’s take this flower as a keepsake from our trip.”

The friends took the beautiful flower as a memory of their mountain journey and happily headed back toward the moving castle. They would always remember this trip as one of their very best adventures, and they never grew tired of discovering the world or of being friends.

The End.
