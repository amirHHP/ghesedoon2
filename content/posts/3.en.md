Once upon a time, in a green and beautiful forest, there lived a little bird named Parpar. Parpar was a happy, playful sparrow who always wanted to fly higher and higher. From the time he was very small, he dreamed of reaching the highest place in the sky and looking out at the whole world from there.

One morning, when the sun had risen and its golden light was shining on the forest, Parpar woke up. He looked at the blue sky and saw other birds flying as far as the farthest point of the sky. He wished he could go up just as high. Excited, he said to himself, "I can do it too! I can go up there too!"

Parpar opened his wings and began to fly. He went higher and higher, but suddenly a strong wind blew and pushed him down. Parpar felt sad and said to himself, "Maybe I cannot go up that high."

But Mama Parpar, who had seen everything, came close to him and said, "Dear Parpar, always try and never give up. You can do it. You just need to practice more."

Parpar decided to practice more every day. He played with his friends, the other birds, and learned from them how to control his wings better. Every morning, Parpar woke up earlier and went flying.

One day, Parpar and his friends — Little Sparrow, Sweet-Voiced Canary, and White Dove — decided to have a race to the tallest tree in the forest. All the birds were excited and got ready.

The race began. Parpar flapped with all his strength and soared toward the sky. He went higher and higher, but a strong wind blew and pushed him down. His friends pulled ahead. Parpar felt discouraged, but then he remembered his mama's words. He flapped his wings more firmly and flew more carefully.

Parpar passed through the white clouds and drew near the tallest tree in the forest. He saw that his friends were close too. With all his strength he gave his last flaps, and at last he reached the highest branch of the tree. From up there he saw the whole forest and his friends, and he was very happy.

His friends joined him, and together they looked down from the top of the tree at the beauties of the forest. Parpar understood that with effort and persistence he could reach anywhere his heart wanted. From that day on, Parpar practiced more and more every day, and he always flew higher than the day before.

Another day, while Parpar was flying, he came to a beautiful rainbow that had appeared in the sky after the rain. He flew over the rainbow and felt that he was closer to his dreams.

With every new flight, Parpar had new stories to tell. He told his friends about the beauties of the sky and encouraged everyone never to give up on their dreams.

One day, Parpar decided to go higher than the highest point he had ever reached. He soared into the sky and flew with all his strength. He passed through the clouds and reached a place where he could see the sun more closely. There he saw that the bigger birds were smiling at him and cheering him on.

Parpar felt that now nothing could stop him. He opened his wings even wider and kept flying, until he reached the highest place he had always dreamed of. From up there, everything looked small, but Parpar's heart felt bigger than ever.

When night came and Parpar returned to his nest, his mama said, "Dear Parpar, you reached your dream and showed that with effort and persistence you can reach anything."

Parpar said with a big smile, "Yes, Mama. I learned that I should never give up on my dreams."

And that is how our little Parpar, with his wishful flight, showed everyone that with hope and effort you can reach any dream. Parpar never stopped trying, and he was always looking for new skies.

The End.
