In a far land, a place where the grasses were always green and the flowers were always colorful, a beautiful spring day began. In this pretty land lived a little girl named Leila. Leila was a brave, curious girl who always explored the world around her and enjoyed the beauties of the spring season.

One day, life in Leila’s land changed. She found her way into a new world full of adventure and both sadness and joy. One day, just as always, Leila went to the forest to look for new flowers and colorful birds. But this time she met a kind little person with a little bird named Parsa.

Parsa was a special bird. He had tails full of rainbow colors that he sent out every time he flew. Parsa was also a good friend, and he and Leila became friends with ease. Together they welcomed the world of spring, and every day they played together and went on adventures.

One day, Leila and Parsa came to a beautiful flower field. Spring flowers of many colors were scattered there, and all around were little bees hopping from flower to flower with energy. Leila and Parsa looked at these beauties with excitement and joy and played together.

But one day, while they were playing in the flower field, the sky grew cloudy and the rain began. Leila and Parsa took shelter from the rain under a big tree. They came closer to each other and, from under the shelter of the tree, looked at the world full of the colors and smells of spring.

A little later, the rain slowly stopped and the sun came. Leila and Parsa came out from under the tree and looked at the blue, shining sky. They understood that even after the rain, life is still beautiful, and spring always goes on with its own beauties.

From that day, Leila and Parsa were friends every day and went on exploring the spring world. They always knew that as they grew older, the spring world full of beautiful colors and smells would still be waiting for them, and they could always enjoy it.

The End.
