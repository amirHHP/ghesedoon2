In a far, far village, where there were still many tales and stories to tell, on a cold winter day, with snow-covered trees and an endless blue sky, the story of the kind fairies began.

In that village there lived a little girl named Sara. Sara wished to have a garden of colorful flowers, and she always played with the birds and the little animals in her garden. But one winter day, a heavy snow covered the ground and turned everything white.

Sara was playing in the garden when suddenly the snow wrapped around her. She said to herself, "How cold it is!" and hurried into the house. But when she looked out her window, her eyes fell on something wonderful.

In the garden, in the very place that had been cold and snowy only hours before, there were now several magic fairies. They flew this way and that in colorful clothes, with magic wings. The first fairy said, "We were very worried about the ground under all this snow, so we came here to help this house and its people."

Sara looked at them with a smile and said, "Thank you for coming! Can you take the snow off my garden?"

The fairies flew around the house and the garden with a big smile, and with their magic movements they lifted all the snow from the flowers and the hanging trees. The garden came alive again, and colorful flowers appeared under the sunlight.

From that day on, Sara was always in touch with the kind fairies, and they helped her whenever she needed it. She was never alone again, because there were always fairies nearby to support her and help her, and this friendship between her and the fairies always stayed.

And that was the story of Sara and the kind fairies, which shows that we should never have to be alone, because magic friends can always be found to help us.

The End.
