In a small, beautiful village, there lived a giant named Tommy. He was a giant, but not such a big one. He was not very tall, and he was not very wide. He was about the size of other giants, but in his heart he wanted to find new friends.

Tommy had a little house beside a river that ran through the village. His house was made of oak wood, and it shone beautifully with the colorful flowers all around it. Tommy loved nature, and every morning he talked with the birds and the other little creatures of nature.

One day, Tommy went to a forest near the village. He liked to look for new flowers and strange, wonderful creatures. While he was exploring, Tommy came upon a small, beautiful bird hanging in a tall tree.

The bird said to Tommy, "Hello, I am the Forty-Feather Bird. What's new?"

Tommy said with a smile, "Hello! I am Tommy, the little giant of the village. You are so beautiful! Would you like to be my friend?"

The bird answered happily, "Of course I would! I like meeting giants. You seem like a very good giant."

A quick friendship grew between Tommy and the Forty-Feather Bird. They talked together, played together, and spent a good time together. Tommy always listened to the bird and helped her whenever she needed it.

One day, Tommy said to the bird, "Can we go looking for a flower? I would so love to find a beautiful flower for my house."

The bird answered happily, "Of course! I know a place where beautiful flowers grow."

Tommy and the bird went looking for flowers together. They found flowers of many colors and many shapes. Tommy chose flowers with bright, happy colors and, with the bird, went back to his house.

He planted the flowers around his house and turned his little house into a beautiful garden. With the help of the Forty-Feather Bird, Tommy brought happiness and beauty back to his village.

As a little giant, Tommy showed that the size of your body does not matter. What matters is your heart and your kindness. And from then on, he went on with his life with friendship and joy, and he showed everyone that a little giant can be great too.

The End.
