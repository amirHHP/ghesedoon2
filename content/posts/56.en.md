Once upon a time, in a small, faraway village, there lived a little girl named Sara. Sara was a kind-hearted, lively girl who always wore a smile and colorful dresses. She loved to play with the flowers in her garden and call to the birds.

One day Sara met an unexpected guest. She was a guardian angel who had cared for Sara since she was a little child. But no one except Sara could see this special angel. Her name was Armina, and she was always beside Sara to keep her safe and happy.

Armina was an angel with shining white wings. She wore blue and pink and always smiled, and she gave Sara a feeling of safety and calm. Sara had never quite known that Armina was there, but she felt that in danger and in hard moments, a protecting power was close by.

One day, while Sara was playing with the flowers in her garden, a familiar voice suddenly reached her ear. Armina came near and said, “My dear, we must get ready for a magic journey.”

Sara asked Armina in wonder, “A magic journey? Where are we going?”

Armina said with a kind laugh, “To a place where anything is possible, to a world we only see in dreams.”

Sara took Armina’s hand with a wide smile, and they went on their journey. They traveled to places full of colors and vanishing magic, to a world of dreams that lives only in the hearts of children.

Armina was always beside Sara. Whenever Sara needed help, she was there. She helped Sara learn to face problems and solve them. With Armina’s help, Sara traveled to lands near and far and learned about the endless beauties of the world.

After their last magic journey, when they came home, Sara felt that she would always have a true friend in Armina. Hoping Armina would always stay close, she fell asleep, and she woke again hoping to see Armina in her dreams.

From that day on, Sara always remembered that she was never alone, because a special guardian angel was beside her, always caring for her and always keeping her safe.

The End.
