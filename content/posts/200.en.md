Once upon a time, in a little village called Cheer, there was a big, beautiful garden full of colorful flowers and fruit trees. This garden was a place where children could play together and enjoy happy moments there.

One sunny day, four friends named Mohammad, Zahra, Ali, and Nazanin decided to go to the garden and swing together. With smiles on their faces, they set off toward the garden.

Mohammad, a brave, energetic little boy, was very happy to have come to the garden. He smiled at his friends and said, “Together we can play interesting games in the garden!”

Zahra, a smart, creative little girl, always loved to play with her friends in open spaces. She said to everyone, “Let’s invent a new game together and have happy moments.”

Ali, a thoughtful, lovable little boy, always helped his friends and always said with a smile, “I always love to play with my friends and make good moments for one another.”

Nazanin, a kind, gentle little girl, always helped her friends, and today too she said to them with joy, “Together we can do anything and make every moment happy.”

In the garden they swung together and enjoyed good moments. They helped one another and treated the flowers and trees of the garden with respect. Together they invented a new game and used everything the garden had to offer.

At the height of their play they reached a beautiful spring whose water was very cool and sweet. The friends drank from the spring with pleasure and told one another how interesting and full of lessons this play in the garden had been. They understood that playing together and helping one another is the best way to joy and happiness.

And that is how, with the beautiful garden full of flowers and trees, Mohammad, Zahra, Ali, and Nazanin always remembered to play together and make a happy time for one another.

The End.
