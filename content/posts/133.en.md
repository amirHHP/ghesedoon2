In a beautiful faraway forest, many different animals lived. Each animal had special skills that made life in the forest easier. But one day, they understood that to stay safe and do well, they needed to share their skills with one another, by working together.

In this forest there was a big, strong bear named Brown. He knew the paths of the forest and always showed the other animals the way. Brown told everyone how to stay away from the dangers of the forest, and this news was very helpful for the other animals.

One day, Brown teamed up with a young leopard named Peli. Peli was very quick and sharp, and he knew how to slip away and hide in the forest. He taught Brown how to climb the trees fast and move from place to place.

In this forest there was also a wise owl named Owly. Owly could see well in the dark and could guide the other animals at night. Owly taught everyone how to give a warning in time when danger was near.

One of the little animals of the forest was a fast rabbit named Harold. Harold was very speedy and could run away from danger in a flash. Harold showed the other animals how to move quickly from one spot to another, and this helped them very much.

But one day, a strong lion named Leo came into the forest. He decided to show his power all by himself and to stop all the animals. He told Brown, Peli, Owly, and Harold how they must try to stay safe, and they all joined together as one.

Each animal used the skills it had to help the others, and together they showed their friendship and teamwork. They learned that every animal has different skills, and by sharing those skills they could live well in the forest. This was a story of the animals' vital skills, and it reminds everyone that with help and kindness, any day can be managed better.

The End.
