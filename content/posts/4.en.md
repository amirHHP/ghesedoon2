Once upon a time, in a faraway world, there was a city called the City of Stories. This city was full of houses, and each house had a strange and magical tale. Every day, when the sun rose, the children woke up and went to the storytelling houses to hear new stories.

In one corner of the city there was a house that told the story of the Rabbit and the Turtle. In this story, a swift rabbit raced a calm, slow turtle. The rabbit thought he was much faster than the turtle and decided to take a little nap in the middle of the race. But when he woke up, he saw that the turtle had quietly come close to the finish line and had won! This story taught the children not to underestimate anyone.

Near that house was another house that told the story of the Princess and the Pea. In this story, a princess slept on many mattresses and blankets, but even so, a tiny pea under the mattresses kept her from sleeping well. This story taught the children that real people notice even the smallest things.

One day, little Arad and his friend Niloufar went to the City of Stories. They decided to look for a story they had never heard before. They came to a house that was telling the story of the Wishing Tree.

In this story, there was a magic tree in the middle of the forest. Anyone who made a wish under it would have that wish come true. But one day, a little boy named Peyman wished under the tree that all the children in the world would be happy and that no one would be hungry. The Wishing Tree granted Peyman's wish, and all the children in the world became happy. This story taught the children that kind, good wishes can change the world.

Arad and Niloufar went home happily and told their friends the story of the Wishing Tree. All the children decided to make good wishes for one another so that their city would always be full of joy and happiness.

At the end of the city there was a house that told the story of the Talking Cat. This cat could speak with people and always had interesting, helpful stories for the children. One of the cat's stories was about a girl named Taraneh who always helped others. Taraneh showed everyone that kindness and helping others can bring you many friends.

Arad and Niloufar went to the City of Stories every day and learned new tales. They understood that every story has an important lesson for life. The stories taught them how to be kind, how to face problems, and how to enjoy the good moments of life.

One day, when Arad and Niloufar went to the City of Stories, they found a new house that was telling the story of the Magic Butterfly. This butterfly could make a rainbow of colors with its flight, and wherever it went, it brought joy and happiness. The butterfly taught the children that life is full of colors and beauties, and that they should enjoy every moment of it.

The City of Stories was always full of the sounds of laughter and joy. With every story they heard, the children learned new things and traveled to a world full of imagination and magic. They understood that stories are not only fun — they can also help them build a better world.

The City of Stories was a place all the children loved to visit, to hear new tales. Every day, at sunrise, the city gates opened and the children ran eagerly toward the storytelling houses. And that is how the City of Stories stayed alive and full of strange and magical stories.

The End.
