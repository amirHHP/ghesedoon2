Once upon a time, in a small and beautiful village, there lived a little girl named Nazanin. Nazanin was a kind and curious girl who loved nature and animals. Every afternoon he went to the forest near the village to talk to the flowers and trees and listen to the birds singing.

One day, Nazanin was walking in the forest when she suddenly heard a strange sound. He listened carefully and saw a beautiful little deer stuck in the bushes. Nazanin hurried to the deer and said, "Don't worry, little one! I'm here to help you."

Deer looked at Nazanin with big eyes full of fear. Nazanin carefully and kindly removed the bushes from the deer's feet and the deer was able to be freed. He looked at Nazanin happily and said, "Thank you! I'm a little deer. You're so kind to help me."

Nazanin exclaimed, "Wow! You can talk? That's amazing!"

The little deer smiled and said, "Yes, I can talk. This forest is full of magic and animals can talk to humans. Would you like to be friends?"

Nazanin happily replied, "Yes, I would love to! I've always wanted an animal friend. We can play together and discover new adventures every day."

From that day on, Nazanin and the little deer became best friends. They ran and played together in the forest every day. The little deer took Nazanin to the secret places of the forest and Nazanin told beautiful stories to the deer.

One day, while Nazanin and the little deer were playing, suddenly a roar was heard in the distance. Nazanin said worriedly, "What is that sound? It seems that something is wrong."

"Let's go see," said the little deer. "Maybe someone needs help."

They went to the sound and saw that a big tree had fallen due to the strong wind and blocked the way of the river. The water of the river was rising fast and could damage the village.

Nazanin said worriedly: "We have to do something! If this water reaches the village, all the houses will be destroyed."

"I have an idea," said the little deer. "Let's go to the forest fairy. She can move the tree by magic."

Nazanin and the little deer ran to the fairy house in the forest. The forest fairy was a kind woman with colorful wings who was always ready to help others. "Forest Fairy, please help! A tree has fallen and blocked the way to the river," Nazanin gasped.

"Don't worry. I'll be right back," the forest fairy said with a smile.

The forest fairy removed the big tree from the river with her magic and the water returned to its course. Nazanin and the little deer happily thanked and returned to the village.

From that day on, Nazanin and the little deer visited the forest fairy every day and learned new and magical things from her. They learned how to be friendly with nature and always help others.

"I'm so glad to have a friend like you, little deer. You showed me how important friendship and helping others is," Nazanin said with a smile.

The little deer also smiled and said, "And I'm very happy to be your friend too, sweetie. Together we can solve any problem and always be together."

And this is how Nazanin and the little deer, the best friends of the forest, always experienced new adventures together and made their world more beautiful by helping others.

The End.
