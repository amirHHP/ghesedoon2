In a small village in the heart of forests full of secrets, there lived a little boy named Armin. Armin, with eyes full of wonder and a heart full of wishes, always looked at the sky and made friends with the shining lights of the stars.

One night, when the sky was full of shining stars, Armin went out alone for the first time into the dark, mysterious forests. He walked toward the tall trees and the deep shadows and met a little magic star who gave him a shining light and a sudden friendship.

The little star named Staryool shone and had come down from the sky. With a sweet smile she said to Armin, "Hello, I am Staryool. I have come from a far world to help the children of the earth." Armin listened to these words with wonder and was happy that he had found a new magic friend.

Staryool took Armin with her on a magic adventure. They leaped into the depths of the forest full of tall trees and flowing rivers. On this path, Armin met magic beings such as talking donkeys, birds with wonderful voices, and flowers with magic colors.

On one of those cold winter nights, when snow had come to the ground, Staryool reminded Armin, "Every winter I come to the children of the world to give them joy and light. You, my friend, will never be alone." Armin listened to these words with joy and felt sure that he would never be far from his friend Staryool.

With the toss of a little stone into the air, Armin promised Staryool that he would always remember her good words and enjoy his magic friendship with her. From that time on, every night Armin looked at the sky with his friend Staryool and found new memories in the forests full of secrets.

This story was helpful not only for Armin but for all the children of the village of Milan as well.

The End.
