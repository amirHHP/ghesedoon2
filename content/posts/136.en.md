Once upon a time, in a faraway forest, birds of many colors and many songs lived together. In this forest the birds held games every day and raced one another to show who had the greater skill.

One of the birds was named Papa, and he had beautiful blue and orange feathers. He was always hardworking and full of energy in the races, and every day he looked for a chance to do his very best flying. Papa also loved to open his wings in the sky and feel free.

Papa joined every race. He was friends with all the birds, but when race time came, he gave all his attention to his flying. One of Papa's friends was Bobo, a little bird with white and black feathers. Bobo was so glad for Papa and always cheered him on.

Every week a race was held. The events included jumping, fast flying, and even flying in loops. Birds from all over the forest gathered to join the races, and it seemed that every bird enjoyed the day.

On one hot summer day, Bobo and Papa joined a new race. This time the race was a jump from a tall tree and a flight around a rock. All the birds came with their friends and families to watch this exciting race.

Then it was Bobo and Papa's turn. They stood side by side, ready to fly. Three... two... one... go! Bobo jumped from the tree with a quick move, and Papa began to fly with his beautiful blue and orange feathers.

Bobo and Papa flew on toward the rock and showed that both of them had very good skills. Bobo with his quick tricks, and Papa with his beautiful, calm moves, amazed everyone who was watching.

At last they both came back to the ground and received congratulations from their friends. This was a special day, a day when all the birds were friends, and they knew better than ever that anyone can be a champion in a race.

This story shows that with trying and working together, we can reach our goals, and that everyone can be a champion, wherever they are.

The End.
