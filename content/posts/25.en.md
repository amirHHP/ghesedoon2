In a small town called Roozegar, there lived three brave children named Ali, Sara, and Milad. They were always looking for adventure and excitement, and they were always searching for hidden treasures.

One day, while they were playing in a big garden, Ali found an old map under the trunk of a tree. It was a map with strange marks that seemed to show a path leading to a hidden treasure in the heart of the forest near the town.

Ali, Sara, and Milad decided together to find this treasure. First they went looking for the marks on the map. They went to the first mark, which was inside a big, beautiful garden full of colorful flowers and tasty fruit.

After that, they set off into a dark forest full of tall, uneven trees. Ali, who was leading the way, found it hard to read the map well. Together they followed different paths that seemed to bring them closer to the treasure.

At last the three children reached a deep, dark valley in the forest. It was a valley from which a cold, flowing river seemed to run below. There they found several marks from the map at the same time. Together they uncovered the secrets and mysteries of that valley.

Following the different paths, they finally came to a big rock that was a sign of the hidden place of the treasure. Ali, Sara, and Milad, with teamwork and togetherness, moved the rock and entered a dark cave full of precious treasures. It was a cave full of gold, jewels, and ancient things that their honesty and togetherness had made into one shared prize.

With a feeling of joy and delight, the three children returned to the town with the treasure they had found. They shared the treasure with everyone else, and all lived in comfort and liveliness. This adventure showed that togetherness and teamwork are always the best way to reach shared goals, and that friendship and cooperation always bring joy and success.

The End.
