Once upon a time, in a green forest full of tall trees, there lived a small, clever mouse named Mooshi. Mooshi was always looking for food and new friends, and because of his clever mind, he always found a way to solve every problem.

One beautiful day, Mooshi decided to look for fresh nuts and seeds for the coming winter. He came carefully out of his nest and began to search the forest. While he was passing among the leaves and branches, he suddenly heard a little cry.

Mooshi followed the sound and saw a little squirrel caught in a trap. The squirrel looked at Mooshi with eyes full of tears and said, “Help me! I am stuck in this trap and I cannot get away.”

Mooshi looked carefully at the trap, and after a little thinking, an idea came to him. He ran quickly to find a long stick and brought it close to the trap. Then, using the stick, he opened the trap and set the squirrel free. The squirrel thanked Mooshi and said, “You are so clever! If you had not been here, I could never have gotten free.”

Mooshi said with a smile, “Friends should always help one another. Now come, let’s look for fresh nuts and seeds together.” And they went looking for food together.

A few days later, while Mooshi and the squirrel were searching for food, they came to a big river. The river was full of water, and the current was very fast. The squirrel said with worry, “How can we cross this river?”

Mooshi thought a little and then said, “We should build a little bridge from sticks and tree branches.” Working together, they gathered sticks and branches and carefully built a small, strong bridge. With the help of this bridge, they were able to cross the river easily.

On the other side of the river, they came to a big tree full of fresh nuts and seeds. Mooshi and the squirrel began to gather food with joy and stored plenty of seeds and nuts for winter.

Another day, while Mooshi was gathering food, he saw that a baby bird had fallen from its nest and could not get back to its home. The baby bird looked around with fear and worry and did not know what to do.

Mooshi came carefully near the baby bird and said, “Do not worry. I will help you get back to your nest.” Then he found a few big, soft leaves and shaped them into a little pillow. The baby bird sat on the soft pillow, and Mooshi carefully carried it back to its nest. The mother bird thanked Mooshi happily and said, “You really are a clever, kind mouse!”

Mooshi said with a smile, “We should all help one another. That is the best way to live.” And then he went on his way.

The days passed, and Mooshi kept helping his friends and finding new ways to solve problems every day. The forest filled with friendship and kindness, and all the forest animals loved Mooshi for his cleverness and his kind heart.

One cold winter day, when all the animals were in their homes, Mooshi remembered last summer and smiled. He remembered all the adventures and all the help he had given his friends, and he felt happy. Mooshi understood that with a clever mind you can always find a way to solve problems and make your friends glad.

From then on, Mooshi was always ready to help any animal who needed him, and the forest was full of joy and friendship. The small, clever mouse always found new ways, and all the forest animals trusted him and loved him.

The End.
