On a beautiful, sunny day, our little friends Mahan and Nika decided to begin a new adventure. They sat in a colorful balloon and were going to travel to faraway lands.

Their balloon rose up and flew into the sky. Mahan and Nika looked down from the balloon and felt wonderful. Slowly and carefully they passed over their town and flew toward the meadows and the mountains.

When their balloon was high up, Mahan said, "Look, Nika! Everything under us looks so small!"

Nika smiled and said, "Yes, it really does! It feels as if we ourselves are moving up here, just like our bigger friends!"

They flew with their balloon toward the rising sun. They flew to far, far lands that no one they knew had ever seen. Mahan and Nika were very happy to see rivers and forests and birds.

For a moment their balloon came closer to the ground, and they used this chance to hop down and look at another place. They flew toward a big forest where the trees were tall and full of tasty fruit.

Mahan said with a laugh, "Look, Nika, how tall these trees are! We can jump up them and touch their fruit!"

Nika smiled and said, "Yes, we can fly wherever we want! This balloon journey is truly magic!"

They flew in the sky for a long time and went on with their journey. They reached other lands full of beautiful flowers and green and yellow meadows. Wherever they went, they discovered a new, beautiful world.

At sunset, Mahan and Nika decided to go home. They returned home with happy memories of the balloon journey and told all their exciting travel stories to their friends.

Their balloon sat down softly on the ground, and they climbed out. This balloon journey to faraway lands was one of their best adventures, and they would always remember this story.

The End.
