In a small, beautiful town there was a big, happy pool that was always full of cheerful, energetic children. This was a place where children swam happily in their colorful swimsuits and enjoyed the water and the sun.

One of these children was a little girl named Sara. Sara was a girl with brown eyes and brown hair who always looked at the world with a smile. She loved swimming, and every time she came to the pool she jumped with joy.

One warm, sunny day, Sara came to the pool with her friends. Her friends were Iman and Reza, two lovable boys with blue eyes and red hair. They always swam together and had interesting games in the water.

Sara and her friends went into the water one after another. The pool water was clear and full of sunlight shining on it. As she swam, Sara made beautiful, gentle movements and enjoyed every bit of it. On her own, with her swimming strokes, she slipped into an underwater world and met shining fish and underwater flowers.

Iman and Reza swam in a race with each other. With fast, energetic strokes they tried to find all the little fish and play with the water. Each of them enjoyed swimming with joy and smiles.

As time passed, all three of them enjoyed swimming and kept going, excited, with their water games. They always knew that the pool was a place where they saw their friends, played with the water, and enjoyed life.

At the end of the day, Sara and her friends went home tired but happy. They always thought with a smile of the good days they had spent at the pool, and they always wished to come back and play their swimming games again.

The End.
