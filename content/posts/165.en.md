On a sunny day, with a blue sky and no clouds, there was a delightful forest full of beauty. In this forest lived tall green trees, colorful flowers, and sweet-singing birds. Every day, new beauties were discovered in this forest.

One day, the sun had painted the sky yellow and orange. This day was very warm, and a crowd of insects and birds searched there for coolness and food. A beautiful butterfly with colorful wings sat on a pretty plant among colorful flowers. With her movements, she showed everyone how beautiful and alive she was.

Another of the animals of this forest was a rabbit. With his long tail and big eyes, he hopped from tree to tree, looking for bananas and greens that grew in the forest under the sunny sun.

In another corner of the forest, there was a little bear who stood up on two feet, looking for tasty, fresh fruits that grew under the forest trees. With his calm, steady movements, he showed everyone how much he loved to wander among the beauties of nature.

Sunny days in the forest were always full of life and happiness. The birds amazed everyone with their singing, and the insects made everyone glad with their dancing and flying. The trees protected everyone from the sun with their shade, and the flowers drew everyone to them with their colors and scents.

Another day, a cool wind blew from the sea toward the forest, and all the flowers and trees began to dance and tremble. This cool wind cooled everyone deep into the forest, and the animals went on wandering through the forest, enjoying the cool air.

At the end of each day, the sun slowly set in the sky, and the earth and sky showed themselves in beautiful, shining colors. These beauties of nature were always in the hearts of all the animals and birds, and they invited them to happiness and joy.

The story reminds children that the beauties of nature always make us glad and happy, and that we should enjoy the beauties of the earth and sky and take care of them so we can always enjoy them.

The End.
