Once upon a time, in a faraway village, there was an ice kingdom built in a land covered with snow and ice. It was a winter land, always full of cold days and snowy weather. In this kingdom a great king named the Ice King ruled, and his people went everywhere in warm, colorful clothes to stay safe from the cold.

One day, a child named Mehrad was born in the village. Mehrad was a boy with a face full of laughter, and he always loved to play in the snow and throw snowballs. He loved the beauty of the ice kingdom and all the people in it.

One cold winter day, while Mehrad was playing in the snow in his yard, something strange happened. He saw a little girl named Parnia in the snow, and to Mehrad she seemed as if she had come from a wizard. Parnia wore colorful clothes and knew beautiful poems, and she was kinder than anyone.

Mehrad and Parnia became friends very quickly. Every day they played snow games together and looked for new adventures among the dancing snow. Sometimes they went farther away to discover the secrets of the ice kingdom, and they met other friends too.

One day, while they were looking for new adventures, Mehrad and Parnia came to a big garden of snow. Inside it stood a large, wonderful doll. This doll could make snow with her magic, and she could even dress the trees and flowers in bright, happy colors.

Mehrad and Parnia looked at these magic wonders with wide eyes, and both of them were glad. Soon they made more friends in the ice kingdom, and every day they all enjoyed life there with snow games and new secrets to find.

From then on, when the Ice King saw the children’s joy and the snowy magic they made in every corner of the kingdom, he always had a smile on his lips. The people of the ice kingdom never lost hope in beauty, and they never stopped keeping one another safe from the cold.

The End.
