Once upon a time, in a small, beautiful village, there lived some children who all loved to play and try new adventures. Among them were three close friends named Arash, Nazanin, and Parham. They always played together and discovered new adventures every day.

One day, while these three friends were playing in the village square, they suddenly heard a strange sound. They followed the sound and saw a magic old man in a long robe and a big hat standing in the middle of the square. The old man smiled and said, “Hello, children! I am a wizard, and I have come to your village to hold a magic race. Are you ready?”

The children cried out with excitement and joy, “Yes, yes! We are ready!” The magic old man said, “Very well. Our magic race has three stages, and each stage is full of challenges and excitement. Anyone who can finish all three stages will be the winner of the race and will receive a special gift.”

The children got ready with happiness and excitement, and the magic old man explained the first stage: “In the first stage, you must find a magic ball hidden in the forest. This magic ball will help you go on to the next stages.”

Arash, Nazanin, and Parham ran quickly toward the forest and carefully looked for the magic ball. They searched among the trees and bushes, and suddenly Nazanin found the magic ball under a big tree. She cried out happily, “I found it!”

The magic old man smiled and said, “Well done. Now that you have found the magic ball, you may go on to the second stage.” He went on, “In the second stage, you must cross the magic bridge that stands above the valley. The magic bridge appears only when you trust it and take a step without fear.”

The children went toward the valley, and when they saw the magic bridge, which seemed almost invisible, they felt a little afraid. But Arash said bravely, “Let’s take a step together and trust the bridge.” They held hands and stepped onto the magic bridge with brave hearts. The magic bridge slowly appeared, and they crossed the valley safely.

The magic old man smiled again and said, “Very well. Now that you have finished the second stage too, you may go on to the third and last stage.” He went on, “In the third stage, you must use the magic ball to solve a magic riddle. If you solve the riddle correctly, you will win the race.”

The magic old man explained the riddle to the children: “The riddle is that you must understand how the magic ball can help you find the magic flowers and bring them to you.” The children thought carefully about the riddle, and Nazanin said, “Maybe if we throw the magic ball toward the flowers, they will come to us.”

Arash and Parham agreed with this idea and threw the magic ball toward the flowers. Suddenly the magic flowers began to shine and came toward the children. The children picked up the flowers, and the magic old man said happily, “Well done! You won!”

The magic old man gave each of the children a special gift. Arash received a magic book, Nazanin a magic necklace, and Parham a magic wand. The old man said, “These gifts will help you always succeed in your adventures and make your friends and family happy.”

The children thanked the magic old man happily and went home. From that day on, using their magic gifts, they tried new, exciting games and always remembered that magic race they finished together.

And that is how Arash, Nazanin, and Parham understood that with courage, trust, and working together they can finish any challenge and always win. The small, beautiful village filled with joy and new adventures, and the children spent happy moments together every day.

The End.
