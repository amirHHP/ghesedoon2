Once upon a time, near a beautiful shore, there lived a little boy named Arman. Arman loved the sea and playing with the sand on the beach. Every day he went to the shore with his mother and father and played for hours with the soft sand. One day, Arman decided to build a big, beautiful castle out of sand.

Arman went to the beach with excitement and, using his bucket and little shovel, began to build a sand castle. Carefully and patiently he made the walls and placed small towers around the castle. After a few hours of work, Arman had built a large sand castle that was beautiful and wonderful.

When Arman finished his castle, a gentle wind suddenly blew and the castle began to shine. Arman was surprised to see that his sand castle had come alive and its doors had opened. Curious, he came closer and went inside. Inside the castle, everything was beautiful and bright. The walls were made of golden sand, and the sunlight made them shine even more.

Arman looked around in wonder and suddenly heard a soft voice say, "Hello, Arman! I am the Prince of Sand. You built my castle, and I want to thank you." Arman looked toward the voice in surprise and saw a small, lovely prince in golden clothes standing before him.

The Prince of Sand smiled and said, "Arman, you built this castle with care and love. As a thank-you, I want to give you a special gift." He gave Arman a small, beautiful box and said, "This box is magic. Whenever you open it, you can come to my sand castle and play with me and my friends."

Arman took the magic box happily and thanked the Prince of Sand. He looked at the castle and said, "This is the best gift I have ever received. I always wanted to have a sand castle, and now I can come here whenever I wish."

The Prince of Sand smiled and said, "Yes, Arman. Whenever you miss the sand castle, just open the magic box and come here. We will always be waiting for you."

Arman left the castle happily and went home. He told his mother and father, "Today I had a wonderful adventure and met the Prince of Sand. He gave me a magic box so I can go to the sand castle whenever I want and play with him."

Arman's mother and father listened with surprise and joy and were glad to see him so happy. Every day Arman went to the beach with excitement and, with his magic box, visited the sand castle. He played with the Prince of Sand and his friends and enjoyed every moment.

Days passed, and Arman and the Prince of Sand became close friends. They told each other interesting stories and had wonderful adventures. Arman learned that with love and care you can make beautiful, wonderful things and enjoy new friendships.

And that is how Arman and the Prince of Sand played in the sand castle every day, and every day was full of joy and adventure. The beautiful shore was full of Arman's laughter and his friends', and all the animals of the beach were happy to see their joy. Arman learned that with love and care you can make the world more beautiful and enjoy new friendships.

The End.
