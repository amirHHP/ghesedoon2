In a small, pretty village, there lived a little boy named Parsa. Parsa was a kind, curious boy who always wanted to play with animals and birds. Every day he played in their garden and enjoyed seeing the flowers and hearing the birds sing.

One day, while Parsa was playing in the garden, he suddenly heard a weak, sad sound from behind the bushes. He walked carefully toward the sound and saw a little bird with colorful feathers lying on the ground, unable to fly. Parsa picked up the bird gently and kindly and carried it into the house.

Parsa said to his mother with worry, “Mama, this little bird cannot fly. We have to help it.”

Parsa’s mother said to him with a kind smile, “My son, we can help it. Let’s make a warm, soft place together so it can rest and get better.”

They filled a little box with soft, warm cloths and put the bird inside. Every day Parsa gave the bird food and water and talked to it. He named the bird Pero, and every day he grew fonder of it.

Pero got better day by day, and Parsa was happy to see that his little bird could fly again. One day, Pero hopped out of the box with joy and flew around Parsa. Parsa smiled and said, “Pero, now you can fly again!”

Pero flew around Parsa happily, as if to say thank you. But instead of flying away, Pero decided to stay in the garden and become Parsa’s good friend. Every day Pero and Parsa played together, and Parsa taught Pero how to find food and take care of himself.

One day, Parsa said to his mother, “Mama, Pero and I are the best of friends. He plays with me every day, and I am so happy I could help him.”

Parsa’s mother smiled and said, “Yes, my son. Friendship with animals and birds is very important. They have feelings too, just like us, and they need love and care.”

Parsa and Pero grew closer every day. Pero made Parsa happy with his singing, and Parsa showed Pero with his love and care how much he mattered. They had many adventures in the garden, and every day they learned new things.

One day, Parsa and Pero decided to go to the forest near the village and see the beauties there. They went to the forest together and enjoyed the tall trees and wildflowers. Pero filled the forest with sweet sounds with his singing, and Parsa listened with laughter and joy.

In the forest, Parsa and Pero found new friends. Pero showed Parsa how to make friends with other birds, and Parsa played with his new friends. They learned that friendship and love can fill their world with happiness and beauty.

The days passed, and Parsa and Pero were still the best of friends. Parsa had learned that friendship with Pero had taught him many things—including love, care, and how important friendship is. He decided always to help animals and birds and to show them love.

And that is how the story of Parsa and Pero came to an end. But their friendship never ended. Parsa and Pero were together every day and enjoyed the beautiful moments of life. They had learned that friendship and love can fill their world with happiness and color, and that they could always be happy together.

Parsa, with a smile on his lips, and Pero, with his sweet song, began each new day, and they knew that their friendship was a real treasure they should always take care of.

The End.
