Let us travel to a far meadow at the edge of the forest, a place where herds of different animals lived. In this meadow many kinds of animals lived: from strong lions to lively leopards and sleepy bears. They all lived in peace and friendship with one another.

One day the sky was blue and clear, and the sun shone from high above. A big, brave lion named Leo, the leader of this group of animals, walked in the meadow with his friends, far from the unkind eyes of hunters. They had gathered together to look after one another and keep safe from dangers that might come.

One day, while the animals were playing and having fun in the meadow, they suddenly heard the familiar sound of a hunter. This hunter was a tricky fox named Freddy, who was looking for a catch. Leo, who had heard stories about Freddy from the old fishing tales, warned everyone to stay away from him.

After telling his friends, Leo decided to make a plan to stand up to Freddy and the other hunters. With courage and togetherness they stood side by side and waited for the hunters. Slowly, Freddy came looking for a hunt in the meadow and set his eyes on them.

Leo told everyone to stay alert. When Freddy reached the nearest trees, Sammy, a little playful squirrel, quickly climbed up into the branches and began to spin and dart about to catch Freddy’s attention.

At the same time, Max, a funny, lively monkey, swung from tree to tree and made funny faces to confuse Freddy and fill him with surprise. On the other side, Ella, a lovable, caring elephant, sprayed water from the river toward Freddy with her trunk and frightened him so that he ran away.

The animals laughed together and enjoyed their victory. From that day on, the animals of the meadow grew even closer, and they were always ready to look after one another and stand up to any danger that might come.

Leo also taught his friends to always stay together and look after one another. From this experience the animals learned that working together and staying close helps them meet hard problems that may come, and in this way they can keep themselves safe.

The End.
