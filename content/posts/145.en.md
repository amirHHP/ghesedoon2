Once upon a time, in a kind little village, there lived a child named Saman. Saman was a playful boy who was always looking for adventure and friendship with animals. One day in the yard of his house, a little sparrow with rainbow feathers came and sat on the branch of a tree.

Saman ran happily toward the sparrow and said in a sweet voice, "Hello, I am Saman. What beautiful feathers you have!"

The sparrow looked at Saman with kindness and joy and said, "Hello, Saman. I am a sparrow. Thank you!"

From that day on, Saman and the sparrow became good friends. They played together in the yard and shared stories with each other. The sparrow told Saman beautiful stories of flying in the sky and spending happy days in nature. Saman told the sparrow stories of his family and friends in the village.

One day, when Saman went into the yard, the sparrow was not there. Saman searched and at last found the sparrow in a corner of the yard. The sparrow was half asleep and seemed to be hurt.

Saman grew worried and came close to the sparrow with care. He saw that the sparrow had a wound. Saman comforted the sparrow and gently wrapped the hurt place. Then he went to his mother so they could make a cozy little spot for the sparrow to get well soon.

After a while, the sparrow got better and began its beautiful, lively flying once more. Saman and the sparrow kept up their friendship and played together every day, happy to see each other.

The friendship of Saman and the sparrow was always special in the kind village. They helped the little sparrow as soon as they could, and this shows how precious friendship and caring for others can be.

The End.
