Welcome to a small village beside forests full of tall trees and rivers with waterfalls. In this village, life was calm and happy, and the children played new games every day beside nature. One of these children was a little boy named Milad who very much wanted to meet the animals of the forest.

One day, while Milad was playing beside a flowing river, a big eagle rose into the sky and flew slowly toward him. The eagle was a large, strong bird, but his eyes were full of calm and peace. It was the first time Milad had met an eagle, and he was very surprised.

The eagle sat down gently beside Milad and said in a soft voice, "Hello, I am Eagle. Would you like to play with me?"

Milad agreed with a big smile, and the eagle showed him his interesting and wonderful gifts. The eagle could fly in the sky and see the world from above the trees. But he also played with Milad on the ground, and they went fishing, jumped over the hills, and looked for wildflowers.

Milad and the eagle soon became close friends. Every day, Milad came back from school and asked the eagle what new news he had from the forest. The eagle told him interesting stories of the forest animals and wild life, and Milad listened with joy.

Once, when one of the other children of the village was lost, Milad went looking for him with the eagle’s help. With his high view and his power of flight, the eagle quickly found the lost child and quickly told Milad. This friendship was precious not only for Milad but for all the people of the village.

From then on, Milad and the eagle were always close friends. Every day after school, Milad spent his time with the eagle beside the river and the forest, and they shared new stories. This friendship showed that a little boy and an eagle can play together and look after each other in everything, no matter how things go!

The End.
