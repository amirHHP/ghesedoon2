In a big, dusty city, the life of the mice was always full of adventure. This city was full of tall buildings and busy streets that were always filled with the noise of cars.

In one of the alleys of this city, a little group of mice lived. These mice were all friends with one another, and they played together every day. Their favorite fun was discovering new and interesting places in the city. For them the main excitement was finding all the different places in the city they could go.

One of the mice was named Milo. He was very curious and was always the first to suggest going to new places. Milo also had a lot of imagination, and every time he went to a new place, he made up new games for his friends.

One day, Milo and his friends decided to go to the city park. The park was one of the mice's favorite places, because there they could play on the green grass and jump up the tall trees.

When the mice reached the park, a little festival was being held. At this festival the mice could enjoy tasty city treats and join in all kinds of games. Milo and his friends joined the festival with excitement and joy and met all the other mice.

But a little mouse named Luca, who had never been with his friends before, was watching from far away. Luca was very shy and had never come to these places. He watched eagerly and wished he could join the other mice.

Milo, who loved to make new friends, came near Luca and said with a smile, "Hello! I am Milo. Do you like the festival flowers too?"

Shy Luca answered with a smile, "Yes, very much! But I am still alone, and I am a little worried."

Milo said to him with a smile, "Do not worry! We are good friends. Come with us and join our group."

Luca joined the other mice happily, and everyone celebrated together. From that day on, Luca always went with his friends to the festivals and new places in the city, and he was always joyful and happy.

This story shows us that with good friends and by sharing good moments, we can fill our lives with joy and happiness every day. The city mice were always good friends, and they never stayed alone. They always helped one another and made one another happy.

The End.
