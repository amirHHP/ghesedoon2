In a small village full of colorful flowers lived a little girl named Niloofar. Niloofar loved colors and magical stories and always thought of sweet, beautiful dreams. Every night when she went to bed, she hoped to see magical dreams.

One night, Niloofar’s kind grandmother sat beside her and said, “Dear Niloofar, tonight I want to tell you the secret of colorful dreams. Every night when you close your eyes and go to sleep, you can travel to a world full of colors and magic. You only have to wish with your heart and think of beautiful things.”

Niloofar said excitedly, “Really, Grandma? How can I travel to that world?”

Grandma smiled and said, “Just close your eyes, think of something you love, and wish with all your heart. The world of colorful dreams will open to you.”

That night, Niloofar closed her eyes and thought of colorful butterflies and beautiful flowers. With all her heart she wished to travel to the world of colorful dreams. Suddenly she found herself in a world full of colors and shining lights. Tall green trees, colorful flowers, and a sky full of bright stars welcomed her.

Niloofar looked around in wonder and enjoyed the beauties of this world. She set off and on the way met a big, shining butterfly. The butterfly came close to Niloofar with her rainbow wings and said, “Hello, Niloofar! Welcome to the world of colorful dreams. I am the Magic Butterfly, and I can go with you in this world.”

Niloofar said happily, “Hello, Butterfly! This place is so beautiful! I want to see everything and enjoy the magic of this world.”

The butterfly smiled and said, “Come with me, Niloofar. I will take you to wonderful places.”

Niloofar and the butterfly set off and reached magical forests. There the trees talked, and the flowers danced to sweet music. Niloofar looked at all this beauty in wonder and enjoyed every moment.

On one of their walks, Niloofar came to a lake whose waters shone in different colors. She came close to the edge of the lake, and suddenly a little shining dolphin came out of the water. The dolphin said in a sweet voice, “Hello, Niloofar! I am the Magic Dolphin, and I can take you to the depths of the sea.”

Niloofar said eagerly, “Hello, Dolphin! I would very much like to go to the depths of the sea and see the underwater world.”

The dolphin put Niloofar on his back, and they went under the water. There Niloofar discovered another wonderful world. Colorful fish played with her, and shining corals welcomed her. Niloofar enjoyed this magical experience very much and felt she was living in a world full of love and joy.

After a while, Niloofar felt tired, and the dolphin brought her back to the surface. Niloofar said to the Magic Butterfly, “This world of colorful dreams is wonderful! I always want to come back here.”

The butterfly smiled and said, “Whenever you wish, just close your eyes and think of beautiful things. The world of colorful dreams is always waiting for you.”

Niloofar went to sleep with a smile and continued her colorful dreams. From then on, every night she went to sleep with eagerness and traveled to her magical, colorful world. These dreams were full of endless adventures and joys, and Niloofar always remembered them with a smile.

The End.
