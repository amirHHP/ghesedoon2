Once upon a time, in a land far from towns and villages, there lived a little boy named Artin. Artin was a brave, kind-hearted child who always loved exploring the forests around his house. Every morning he woke up early and, with a basket of dates and cheese, walked the dirt roads toward the trees.

One day, Artin went toward a deep part of the forest. This part of the forest was full of tall trees that made a covering shade. Artin was looking for wildflowers to bring to his grandmother. While he was running among the trees, he suddenly heard a bearlike sound. Artin trembled with fear at first, then slowly turned his head and saw a young bear with brown eyes and soft brown fur standing before him.

The young bear stared at Artin. Because it had never seen a person before, it felt happy and amazed. Artin, too, was moved by the beauty and bravery of the young bear. He came closer, a little shy, and held out his hands toward the bear. The young bear was still worried, but it came near Artin and smelled the fruit in the basket.

For a moment Artin stepped back from the shy, lovable young bear. After a short time, Artin spoke: "I am Artin. I am glad to meet you." The young bear still stared at Artin, but then it smiled too. From that moment, the friendship of Artin and the young bear began.

Artin and the young bear spent time together every day. They played together, learned together, and hid themselves in the forest. Artin taught the young bear about wildflowers and the animals of the forest, and the young bear showed Artin how to climb the tall trees.

As time passed, the friendship of Artin and the young bear grew stronger. They were side by side at all times and supported each other in the hard days and the happy days. In this way, Artin's life in the forest, with a young bear for a friend, was very sweet and full of wonder.

The End.
