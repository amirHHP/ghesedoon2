Once upon a time, in a world full of color and joy, the life of a butterfly named Colorful began. Colorful was a butterfly whose wings shone in many colors: red, blue, yellow, and purple. She lived in a garden full of colorful flowers and high places covered with green, beautiful plants.

As a very special butterfly, Colorful was always proud that she could give color and beauty to the world around her. She flew through the garden, and with every bunch of flowers she saw, she sipped the sweet nectar hidden in the heart of the blossom.

One day the sky grew cloudy and a strong wind began to blow. The wind tossed the leaves and shook the flowers. Colorful and her butterfly friends were playing and having fun in the garden, but suddenly the wind grew stronger. Colorful rose up from everywhere at once and danced in the sky.

The strong wind tossed the butterfly far away, and along the way Colorful bumped into a tree and soon fell to the ground. She tried hard to lift herself from the earth and fly, but the butterfly was hurting and could not move easily.

At that moment, a little boy named Matthias, who was playing in the garden, saw Colorful. He came near and carefully picked up the butterfly. Colorful rested gently in Matthias’s hand and felt safe.

Matthias looked at the butterfly carefully and gazed in wonder at the beauty of Colorful’s wings. He said, “Oh my! How beautiful this butterfly is! Her wings have all my favorite colors.”

After a moment, Matthias held the butterfly carefully in his hands and slowly went back across the ground. He carried Colorful to a pot of flowers and set her on a blossom. Colorful sat calmly on the flower and felt that everything in the world would be all right.

From that day on, Matthias came to the garden every day and said hello to Colorful. He told his friends about Colorful’s beauty, and every time they saw her they were glad. Colorful always reminded Matthias that anyone can make the world better and brighter with color and beauty. All you need is to love others with an open heart and treat them with care.

In this world full of color and joy, Colorful and Matthias remembered this lesson every day and enjoyed a good friendship together.

The End.
