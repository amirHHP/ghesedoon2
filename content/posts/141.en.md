Welcome to a faraway forest full of many different animals. In this forest a beautiful, adventurous life went on for the animals who lived there. Every animal had a family and lived with that family.

In one corner of the forest lived a family of birds. The birds, with their beautiful, colorful feathers, always flew into the sky and had a happy time together. The mother and father birds taught their baby, a little one named Pippa, how to fly and how to use tasty foods for meals.

On the other side of the forest lived a family of rabbits. The rabbits, with their big ears, quickly warned one another of danger. The mother and father rabbits taught their children, Happy and Hoppy, how to dig little holes in the ground and how to stay safe from enemies.

In another part of the forest lived a family of foxes. The foxes, with gray coats and fluffy, nut-brown tails, were always looking for food. The mother and father foxes taught their children, Rudy and Rita, how to hunt well and how to take care of the beautiful world of nature.

Every family in the forest had a special job, and each one taught its children, in turn, how to live in this great forest. They always helped one another and never stayed far apart. They were always smiling and happy, and they always treated one another with respect.

This story shows that every family in the forest has a special place, and that each of them should live together and care for one another.

The End.
