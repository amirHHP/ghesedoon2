In a far, beautiful land, there was a small village called Shadestan. In this village lived a kind, happy child named Arash. Arash always played with his friends and enjoyed new adventures. But he always had one wish: to become friends with a dragon.

One spring day, Arash decided to go to the big forest behind the village. He had heard that a kind dragon lived in that forest. With great excitement he took a few small things from home and set off. He reached the forest and looked carefully around. The forest was full of tall trees and colorful birds, but there was no sign of a dragon.

Arash felt a little discouraged and decided to rest. He sat in the shade of a big tree and took an apple out of his bag. Suddenly he heard a strange sound. He listened carefully and saw that something was moving behind a big tree. With a little fear and excitement, he went toward the sound.

When he came closer, he saw a small, beautiful dragon eating forest berries. The dragon had shiny green skin and bright eyes. Arash carefully came near the dragon and said, "Hello, I am Arash. Who are you?"

The dragon looked at Arash in surprise and said, "Hello, Arash! I am Diana. What are you doing here?"

Arash said with a smile, "I have always wished to become friends with a dragon. Will you be my friend?"

Diana answered with a warm smile, "Of course I will be your friend, Arash. I have always wanted to become friends with a human too."

From that moment, Arash and Diana became the best of friends. Every day Arash went to the forest and played with Diana. They went to new places in the forest, climbed the tall trees, and swam in the little river. Arash always brought tasty treats for Diana, and Diana cooked for Arash with her fiery flames.

One day, while Arash and Diana were playing, they heard a cry from far away. They went toward the sound and saw that a baby deer was caught in a trap. Arash quickly said to Diana, "We must help this baby deer!"

Diana flew with her big wings and went toward the trap. Carefully and with strength, she broke the trap and set the baby deer free. The baby deer thanked Diana and Arash happily and ran toward its mother. Arash looked at Diana with pride and said, "We are a great team!"

Diana answered with a smile, "Yes, Arash. We are the best of friends."

The days passed quickly, and the friendship of Arash and Diana grew stronger every day. Together they learned how to face problems and help others. Arash always told his friends in the village the stories of his adventures with Diana, and everyone was proud of this wonderful friendship.

One winter day, when snow had covered everything, Arash and Diana decided to go to the village and celebrate with the people. Diana flew with her big wings and seated Arash on her back. When they reached the village, all the people looked at Diana with wonder and excitement. At first they were a little afraid, but when they saw that Arash was friends with Diana and that she was a kind dragon, everyone welcomed her with smiles and happiness.

That night, the people of the village held a great feast. They got to know Diana and welcomed her as a new member of the village. Diana amazed everyone too by showing her abilities, such as lighting a fire to warm the people and flying in the sky.

That night Arash and Diana understood how important their friendship was to everyone. They learned that with love and teamwork they can turn the world into a better place. From that day on, Diana lived among the people as the protector of the village and as Arash's loyal friend.

This story shows that with a kind heart and true friendship, we can overcome all problems and build a better life for ourselves and others. Arash and Diana were always together and experienced new adventures side by side, because true friendship never ends.

The End.
