In one of the small villages of Iran, there lived a little boy named Sam. Sam was a very curious and lovable boy. He always looked at the sky and wondered how he could go into space and see the stars and the planets.

One day, Sam said to his friends, a girl named Parya and another boy named Arman, "I want to go into space! Can I?" Parya and Arman looked at him with a laugh and said, "Yes, we can go into space together!"

After that, Sam and his friends decided to travel into space. They got in touch with an astronaut they knew and talked with him about the space journey. The astronaut helped them get their travel plan ready and showed them how to go to the spaceship.

The journey into space began, and the spaceship carried them toward the moon and the other planets. Sam and his friends were very happy to see the views of space and the shining stars. Together they sat and watched Earth from far away, and they felt that everything under their feet was small.

During the journey, Sam and his friends reached the moon. They walked on the surface of the moon and got to know that extraordinary world. Through a space telescope they explored and studied the moon, and they enjoyed its beautiful views.

On the last night of the journey, Sam and his friends traveled to a far point in space from which they had a beautiful view of Earth and the stars. Because of this special and beautiful journey into space, they gained an unforgettable memory of the new and beautiful world of space.

Sam and his friends' return to Earth was full of happiness and joy. They went back to their homes and told their families and friends wonderful stories of the journey into space. Sam said to his family, "I can do anything I want! Even go into space!"

From then on, Sam and his friends would always remember that special and adventurous journey into space, and every day they would go on with their lives, hoping to discover a new world and the vastness of the universe.

The End.
