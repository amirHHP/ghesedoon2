On a sunny and hot day, the children of the village, together with their teacher, go on a fascinating trip to a volcanic area. They are amazed by these natural beauties and hear an interesting story about the life of volcanoes and their effects.

The children named Anna and Mohammad, together with their friends, start their journey under the leadership of their beloved teacher, Mrs. Leila. They started their journey from a green and beautiful village and headed towards a high volcanic mountain known as "Aryan Volcanic Mountain".

When they reached the top of the mountain, they saw a wonderful view around them. "Volcanoes are like the beautiful feathers of the earth that can keep nature alive!" Anna told her friends.

Mrs. Leila approached the children with a big smile and began to explain a story about the life of volcanoes. "Volcanoes are the earth like a heart that has been sleeping inside the earth and when they wake up, they can cause fires and big earthquakes," he said.

Muhammad listened carefully and then asked a question: "Can volcanoes produce other things besides fire and earthquakes?" "Yes, Muhammad. Volcanoes can make new rocks and minerals, and sometimes, they can open up the soil and help plants grow," Miss Leila answered with a smile.

The children all listened and then continued climbing and exploring volcanoes together. They learned how volcanoes have huge effects on the growth of nearby plants and animals and how they keep these forests and plains alive.

After this beautiful trip, the children returned home with new experiences and every night they remember the stories of the volcano and their lives together, when the earth sleeps and the volcanoes sleep, for this purpose.

The End.
