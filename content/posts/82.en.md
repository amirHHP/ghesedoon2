In a faraway, beautiful world, there was a magic garden that filled every child with wonder. This garden was full of colorful flowers and magic creatures, and you could feel them in every corner.

A little girl named Anisa always loved this garden very much. Every day after breakfast she went to the magic garden to play with her friends, the colorful birds and the magic butterflies. In this garden you could see flowers of every color you wished. There were yellow flowers, blue flowers, red flowers, and even purple ones whose sweet smell made every child feel fresh and happy.

One day, in the magic garden, Anisa came upon a big old tree. This tree was full of sweet, colorful fruit. The tree said to Anisa in a lovely voice, "Hello, dear Anisa. I am so glad you came to see me."

Anisa said in wonder, "Hello, magic tree! What beautiful fruit you have. May I pick one?"

The magic tree smiled and gave Anisa a yellow, sweet fruit. "This is one of my fruits. Eat it and see what magic happens."

Anisa ate the fruit, and at that very moment she felt energy and happiness fill her body. This fruit was truly magic!

After that, Anisa came to the magic garden every day and played with her new friends, the flowers and the magic creatures. She became friends with birds who could talk, and with butterflies who could change color. Every day the magic tree gave her a new fruit, and it was always a surprise for Anisa.

And from then on, the magic garden became Anisa's best friend. She always came there to enjoy its beauties and its magic, and to spend happy times with her friends and the magic creatures.

The End.
