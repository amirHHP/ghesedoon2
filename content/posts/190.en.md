Once upon a time there was a magic scarf in a faraway village, and it belonged to a boy named Milad. This scarf was not like any other scarf. It had a special magic power that turned cold winter days into a pleasant kind of magic.

Milad was a young boy with an energetic spirit. He always traveled into a world of adventures and wonders with his magic scarf. When he wrapped the magic scarf around his neck, amazing things happened.

One cold winter day, Milad went to a snowy park with his magic scarf. When he put the magic scarf on, a wonderful magic took place, and Milad entered a world of beautiful, delightful toys. He built big snowmen with his friends and played in the snow. Wherever he went, with the magic power of his scarf he found interesting and wonderful things.

With the magic scarf, Milad could enter a world of colors and wonders. Every time he used the magic scarf, he felt as if he had a loyal magic friend. This scarf was not only a piece of clothing for winter warmth. It was a magic map that guided Milad into a world of unknown beauties.

When the sun drew near the earth and sent its light and warmth to Milad and his friends, he went home happily, and with his life with the magic scarf he carried beautiful memories down into sleep.

The End.
