Once upon a time, in a faraway land, there was a small, beautiful village beside a great forest. The forest was full of tall trees and colorful birds. But what made the village different from other places were the big, playful giants who lived in the forest. Unlike the scary giants in stories, these giants were very kind and playful, and they always loved to play with children.

One morning, Arya and her friends, Leila and Saman, decided to go to the forest and play with the playful giants. They had always heard from the grown-ups that the giants were very kind and played with all the children. So they set off toward the forest together.

When they reached the forest, they heard laughter and play in the distance. It was the sound of the giants playing. Arya said excitedly, "Let's go play with them!" Her friends agreed and ran toward the sounds.

They soon came to a large green field where the playful giants were playing. The giants were so big that when they laughed loudly, their voices filled the whole forest. One of the giants, whose name was Gorgor, came up to the children and said kindly, "Hello, children! Welcome to our forest. Would you like to play with us?"

Leila said excitedly, "Yes, we would love to!" Saman said, "We have heard that you are very kind and have interesting games."

Gorgor laughed and said, "Yes, we love games. Let's play together. We have a very fun game called hide-and-seek. Would you like to play it?"

The children happily agreed, and the game began. With their big hands, the giants could easily hide behind the tall trees. The children had to find them. The game was very exciting, and everyone laughed and had a wonderful time.

After hide-and-seek, it was time for the high-jump game. With their long jumps, the giants could leap to the tops of the trees. The children tried to jump as high as they could with the giants' help. Gorgor said kindly, "Come, I will lift you so you can jump higher."

With Gorgor's help, Arya and her friends jumped very high, and the experience was thrilling for them. After the high jumps, it was time to build sand castles. The giants made big, beautiful sand castles with their large hands, and the children helped them.

Saman smiled and said, "This is the best day of my life. I am so glad we met you."

Leila said excitedly, "We will always come here to play with you. You are our new best friends."

The giants were very happy with this new friendship too. Gorgor said, "We are very happy to have you as well. Always come here and play with us."

When the sun began to set, the children had to return to the village. The giants walked with them to the edge of the forest and smiled. "We will be waiting for you. Come again whenever you like."

Arya, Leila, and Saman went back to the village with hearts full of joy and beautiful memories, and they told their families about their adventures. From that day on, the children always went to the forest and played with the playful giants, and this kind, beautiful friendship went on.

In that faraway land, the village beside the forest was always filled with the laughter and joy of the children and the playful giants, and everyone lived happily together.

The End.
