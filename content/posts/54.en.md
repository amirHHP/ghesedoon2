Once upon a time, in a small, faraway village, there lived two little friends named Leila and Milad. Leila was a creative, lively girl who was always looking to make and invent new things. Milad was a clever, lovable friend who always wore a bright smile and was always thinking.

One day, Leila came upon something wonderful. She went to the house of a kind old woman with a sweet face, whose name was Nana. Nana was an artist, and in almost no time at all she made beautiful, magical paintings. Nana’s paintings had a special power. They could make wishes come true!

Leila looked at Nana’s paintings with eyes full of wonder. She knew these paintings could solve problems and turn all kinds of wishes into something real. Nana taught Leila that anyone who wants to make something needs creative ideas. She said, “Never give up on yourself, and always keep going to find what you love in life.”

One day, Leila said to Nana, “I want to paint a picture where all my friends can see everything they love.”

Nana smiled and said, “Well then, you will truly believe you are making a wish painting. But remember: you must know your friends’ wishes.”

Leila went eagerly to her friends and asked them about their wishes. Milad said he wanted to build a castle with big windows and a library full of books. Leila told Nana this wish, and Nana painted a beautiful castle with a big library and big windows.

Then Leila went to Susan, who wanted to go to a world full of colorful flowers and beautiful birds. Nana turned that wish into a painting for Leila too. Nana’s paintings made the friends’ wishes come true, and everyone was happy.

From that day on, everyone came to Nana to see the paintings of their wishes. Leila, Milad, and Susan learned from Nana’s magic paintings how to turn their wishes into something real, and they stayed happy always.

The End.
