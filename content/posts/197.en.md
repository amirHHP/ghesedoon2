One warm, sunny day, in a little town called Happytown, little friends named Mahin, Sara, Ali, and Niloufar went to the neighborhood park. They always loved to play in the park, and today they were having a good time together too.

Mahin, a brave, energetic little boy, had come with skates for the first time. He stood on his skates and showed his friends how to skate in the park. Sara, a smart, creative little girl, smiled at Mahin and said, “I want to skate too!”

Sara picked up her own skates and moved toward Mahin. Ali, a thoughtful, kind little boy, joined his friends with skates as well. They all remembered that they should be careful and look after one another.

Niloufar, a gentle, kind little girl who always loved to help everyone, said to Mahin, Sara, and Ali, “I’m coming too! I love skating so much!”

The friends looked after everyone and always helped one another. With their skates they learned new skills in the beautiful green park and enjoyed a happy time together.

After a while, Mahin said to his friends, “Let’s have a race! Let’s see who can skate the most laps.”

Everyone joined the race with excitement. Mahin was the first to skate quickly and complete many laps on the skating path. Sara and Ali kept trying too and finished many laps. Niloufar also skated many laps with joy and patience and showed everyone how hard she had tried.

At the end of the race, they all high-fived and shared lots of laughs. They understood that it does not matter who won. What matters is playing together and making a happy time for one another.

After the race, the friends told everyone how to skate safely and enjoy the park together. They always remembered that playing together and helping one another is the best thing they can do.

And that is how, with their skates, Mahin, Sara, Ali, and Niloufar always remembered that loving and helping is the best way to joy and happiness.

The End.
