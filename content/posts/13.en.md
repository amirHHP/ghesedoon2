In a small, pleasant town called Pargol, there lived a cheerful, lovable little boy named Armin. Armin was a curious and kind boy who always said hello to everyone around him with a smile and was good to everyone.

Armin loved to go to the park with his friends and play. He played in the park with a girl named Parya and a boy named Amir. They always went close to the flowers and gave them water.

One day, Armin and his friends went to a garden full of flowers and tasty fruit. Together they watered the flowers and enjoyed nature. While they were playing, Armin saw a new boy named Mehran. He was alone and crying.

Armin went to Mehran with his friends and asked him why he was crying. Mehran said, "I am alone and I have no friend." Armin said to Mehran with a smile, "We are good friends, and we will always be by your side."

Armin, Parya, Amir, and Mehran always played together in the park and the gardens of the town. With smiles on their faces, they watered the flowers, talked with the trees, and cared for the little animals in the park. A new day came for them every day, and their friendship grew stronger each day.

In this small, lovable world, Armin and his friends showed that loving others and sharing happy moments is one of the greatest gifts of life. They always showed that you can always help one another learn from each other.

The End.
