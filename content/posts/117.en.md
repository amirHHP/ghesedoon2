In the muddy forests full of secrets, there lived a little boy named Milo. Milo was a kind-hearted, brave child who was always looking for adventure and discovering new things. Every day he walked in the forest looking for strange animals and said hello to them.

One day, while Milo was searching for a hidden treasure at the edge of the forest, a big, kind dinosaur named Dino found him. Dino was a dinosaur who had come to the forest from a time long, long ago. He stood unexpectedly beside Milo and looked at him with kind eyes.

It was the first time Milo had seen a dinosaur, and he was so full of wonder and joy that he could not say a word. But Dino came close to him with a wide smile and said, "Hello, I am Dino. What is your name?"

Milo was still amazed and surprised, but he answered with a smile, "I am Milo. I am happy to meet you, Dino."

From that day, Milo and Dino became good friends. They always went together looking for adventure and new discoveries. Dino told Milo many stories from the time of the dinosaurs, and Milo listened with wonder.

One day, when they were beside a big tree in the forest, Dino reminded Milo, "Milo, you are so brave. Do you know that a friendship like yours is very precious to a dinosaur like me?"

Milo came close to Dino with a smile and said, "Yes, Dino. You are a big, good friend. I always love to be with you and enjoy new experiences together."

Dino laughed too and said, "I have always been looking for a friend like you, Milo. You always surprise me with your bravery and your bright ideas."

From that day, Milo and Dino were always together. They played in the forest, enjoyed nature, and always set off together for new adventures. Their friendship stood up to every worry and every hard moment, and both of them were very happy to have each other.

And so the friendship of Milo and Dino showed that even two beings who are very different can be the very best of friends as a team, and always look after each other.

The End.
