In a small, faraway town, there lived a little boy named Armin. Armin was a boy who always liked to surprise the people around him with his play and his adventures. He loved to go exploring and play with whatever he found — including the wind.

One day Armin went to a little garden beside his house. It was a garden full of trees and beautiful flowers, and Armin always came there to play with his friends, like the birds and the butterflies. But that day, Armin discovered a new game: playing with the wind.

Armin sat behind a tree and carefully felt the soft, cool breezes of the wind on his skin. He decided to play with the wind. Armin stretched out his hand to catch the wind and enjoyed it. The wind laughed softly and gently against Armin's hands and invited him to play.

Armin began a new game with the wind. With his hands he caught the wind and moved it up and down. The wind laughed and laughed and enjoyed playing with Armin. Armin felt that he and the wind were becoming good friends, and that they could always play together.

After the game, Armin sat behind the tree and looked at the clear blue sky. He thought how beautiful and pleasant it was to play with the wind, and what a good friend the wind could be. Armin made a lovely story in his mind about friendship with the wind, and he promised himself that he would play with the wind every day and enjoy this good friendship.

In this story, Armin and the wind show that sometimes new friends, like the wind, can be very important and lovely for us, and we can enjoy being friends with them.

The End.
