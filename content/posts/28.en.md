Once upon a time, in a small, charming village called Roozgard, there lived a little girl named Anya. Anya was a curious, kind girl who always helped the village animals and talked with them.

One day, Anya went to a big tree near the river. She sat beneath the tree and listened to the sound of the water. Just then, a rabbit named Happy joined her and said, “Hello, Anya! What’s new?”

Anya smiled and answered, “Hello, Happy. I’m well. How are you?”

Happy said, “I’m well too, but today I want to find some carrots, and I don’t know where to go.”

Anya smiled and said, “Come with me. I’ll help you.”

Together they went to the friendly carrot garden. Anya helped Happy find tasty carrots, and they picked a few for themselves as well.

After that, Anya walked toward a spring in the forest. There she found a bear named Brown, who kept the peace of the woods. Brown welcomed Anya and said, “What’s new, Anya?”

Anya said happily, “Hello, Brown. I’m looking for a nice place to sit and eat.”

Brown smiled and said, “You can sit right here. I have a good spot for resting.”

Anya sat under a big tree beside the spring and talked with Brown. She shared lovely stories about the village and the animals, and Brown told Anya wonderful stories about the forest and life there.

After a while, Anya went to the big tree in the heart of the village. A little mouse named Milo was waiting for her. Milo welcomed Anya to the village with joy and said, “I found a new hiding place today!”

Anya said in wonder, “Really? Show me!”

Milo took Anya to his hiding place and showed her how she could use it too.

Anya had become very good friends with the animals of the village and the forest. She always helped them and talked with them. She lived with joy and happiness, and all the animals were glad of her friendship and kindness.

In the days that followed, Anya still talked with the animals and helped them. She was always a good friend to everyone, and everyone was very happy with her.

The End.
