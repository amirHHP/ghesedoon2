Once upon a time, in a faraway world full of magic and wonders, a few little children named Anna, Max, and Sara lived. Every day from morning until night they played, and they gathered many memories of happiness and joy. But one day, while Anna was looking carefully at an old book, a line of magic letters appeared on the pages. The letters began a story of time travel for them.

“Anna, Max, and Sara,” it read, “with your little hands you will travel into a magic story.”

The children looked at one another in wonder and believed the letters. At that moment, everything around them grew dark, and they felt as if they were birds coming down to the earth. When they opened their eyes, they were standing in a green meadow full of colorful flowers. Around them there was nothing but this sweet-smelling, beautiful meadow.

“Woooow!” Sara cried. “Where are we?”

Anna looked around quickly and said, “I think we are in a magic meadow!”

Max went to a red rose and smelled it. “It smells so good!”

At that moment, a wise old person came near them. He said with a lovable smile, “Welcome to the world of the past. This is a place where anything is possible.”

The children looked at the wise one in wonder. He explained that they had come to learn and explore in this world of the past. Anna, Max, and Sara each told him they wanted to see and learn many things.

After some time spent learning and exploring, the children slowly returned to their own world. They enjoyed their adventure, and every day after that passed with even more joy. From then on, whenever Anna, Max, and Sara looked at their old book, they believed a world of adventures and exciting tales was waiting for them.

In this children’s world, anything is possible, even traveling through time and learning from the past.

The End.
