On a sunny spring day, on a small, cozy farm, there lived a spirited rooster named Coco. He was a bright, lively rooster who always gazed at the colors and beauty of spring mornings.

On spring mornings, Coco woke up and, with his song, woke everyone else. Coco’s beautiful, spirited crowing gave energy to all the farm animals. With his loud voice he woke them and welcomed them to a day full of energy and joy.

Coco always gave energy to his friends, the ducks and the hens of the farm, and invited them to play and go on adventures. Every day they all played games together, and the sound of their laughter and joy reached everyone around them.

One day, while Coco was having breakfast, he heard that a spring festival would be held in town. Coco smiled a big smile, told his friends, and together they decided to go to the festival and show others their joy, dancing, and spirit.

When they reached the town, all the farm animals were welcomed happily to the festival. Among the joy of the people around them, Coco and his friends amazed everyone with their dancing and singing. They showed how, with energy and cheer, they could turn every day into a bright spring morning.

When the festival ended, Coco and his friends went home, carrying sweet memories and happy, energetic moments from that day. They would always remember that together, with positive energy, they can turn every day into a happy day full of adventure — just like Coco and his friends in this story.

The End.
