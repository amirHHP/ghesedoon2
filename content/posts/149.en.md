In a faraway village, life moved slowly, and every new sunny day brought joy to the children who lived there. One of the children of this village was named Leila. Leila was a little girl who always played with the birds and knew them well.

One spring day, when the flowers were shining and the birds were happy, Leila met a special bird. This bird was a bird with white feathers. Its bright white feathers were like snow, shining and sweet to see. Leila looked at the bird carefully and then tried to find a name for it.

"I will call you White!" Leila said to the bird with a happy laugh.

After that, the white bird and Leila became friends. Every day the white bird played with Leila and flew freely in the sky. With its white feathers it was like an angel sent from the sky to play with the children of the village and make them happy.

But one day the sky grew cloudy and rain began to fall. Leila worried that the white bird might catch a chill or be hurt by the rain. She quickly looked up at the sky and searched for the white bird. But the white bird was not there.

Leila ran quickly to her little house in the village. She looked for the white bird, but it was nowhere. Leila's heart felt lonely, and the rain began to fall a little more.

In that moment, a soft, sweet sound reached Leila's ears. She looked and found the white bird shining in a dry corner. The white bird came close to Leila and carefully shook its feathers to shake the rain away.

"White, I was so worried!" Leila said with a happy smile.

The white bird gently tucked its wings and came close to Leila. In a soft voice it said to Leila, "I am always here, Leila. Rain comes and rain goes, but our friendship will always stay."

Leila gently hugged the white bird and went home in peace. From that day on, the white bird was always with Leila and stayed with her on good days and hard days. They had a good friendship and never left each other.

The white bird, with its white feathers, was always a keepsake of the close friendship that Leila and the bird had made together.

The End.
