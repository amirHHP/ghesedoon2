Once upon a time, in a green meadow full of flowers and nightingales, there lived a little boy named Arman. Arman was a happy, energetic boy who always loved to play and run with his friends in the meadow.

Early one morning, Arman and his friends, Sam and Sara, came to the meadow to play and run. The meadow was full of colorful flowers and tall green trees. The sky was blue and clear, and the sun was slowly coming out from behind the mountain.

Arman and his friends began to run. They kept up with one another and, with smiles on their faces, looked for new adventures in the meadow. Arman was the first to start running, then Sam and Sara came after him. They both caught up with Arman and got busy playing.

Running in the green meadow was a great joy for Arman and his friends. They ran together across the meadow, chased one another, and while they ran they enjoyed the beauty of nature. The flowers along the way gave them a pretty scent, and the birds flying above them sang happy songs.

As time passed, Arman and his friends grew tired and sat down to rest. Just like that, in the green meadow, they were together and shared happy moments. The sky was still blue, and the sun was slowly moving toward sunset.

Playing and running in the green meadow was a good, happy day for Arman and his friends. They played, laughed, and spent good moments together. When the sun drew near to setting, they went home, each carrying a beautiful memory of running in the green meadow and the joy they had taken from nature.

And that is how another day in the green meadow came to an end, but Arman and his friends would always remember how they ran in the beautiful green meadow and enjoyed their happy moments.

The End.
