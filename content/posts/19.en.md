In a faraway land, in a big, beautiful castle, there lived a little princess named Nazanin. Nazanin was a kind, warm-hearted girl, and all the people of her land loved her. She loved nature and animals, and she always played in the castle garden with the flowers and the birds.

One sunny day, Nazanin decided to go to a beautiful lake near their castle. She picked up a little basket of bread and tasty fruit and set off toward the lake. When she reached the lake, she sat under a big tree and enjoyed the beautiful views around her.

Suddenly a faint voice came from among the reeds. Nazanin went toward the sound with curiosity and saw a little green frog sitting on a water-lily leaf, looking as if it wanted to say something. The frog said to her, "Hello, Princess Nazanin! I am a magic frog. Can you help me?"

Nazanin asked in surprise, "How can I help you, little frog?" The frog went on, "I am really a prince who was turned into a frog by a wicked wizard. The only way for me to return to human form is for a kind princess with a pure heart to kiss me."

Nazanin said kindly to the frog, "I would very much like to help you." Then she carefully and gently picked up the frog and gave him a little kiss. Suddenly a shining light surrounded the frog, and he turned into a young, handsome prince.

The prince said happily to Nazanin, "Thank you so much, Princess Nazanin! You saved me from this spell. I am Prince Arman, and I will be grateful to you forever."

Nazanin and Prince Arman decided to go back to the castle and tell the king and queen about the magical adventure. When they reached the castle, everyone was surprised to see Prince Arman, and they were happy that Nazanin had been able to save him.

The king said with a smile, "My dear Nazanin, you showed that you have a heart full of kindness and love. I am proud of you." The queen hugged Nazanin too and said, "You are the best girl in the world."

From that day on, Prince Arman and Princess Nazanin became the best of friends. They played together in the castle garden and told each other wonderful stories. Prince Arman taught Nazanin how to face challenges with courage and bravery, and Nazanin showed Arman how to live with a heart full of love and kindness.

Years passed, and Nazanin and Arman grew older. They were still the best of friends, and together they spread peace and happiness in their land. The people of their land always remembered them as a symbol of kindness and courage.

One day, the king and queen decided to hold a great feast in honor of Nazanin and Arman. All the people of the land were invited to the castle, and a great feast was held with music, dancing, and tasty food. At this feast, the king announced that Nazanin and Arman had been chosen as the future prince and princess of their land.

Nazanin and Arman looked at the people with joy and smiles and promised that they would always rule with hearts full of love and kindness. And so their faraway land was always full of peace, happiness, and joy.

The story of Nazanin and the magic frog shows that with kindness and love you can overcome any challenge and build a world full of beauty and happiness. And this story always remained in the memories of the people of their land.

The End.
