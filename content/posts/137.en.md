Once upon a time, in the heart of a green forest, there lived a little child named Milo. Milo was a kind-hearted, energetic child who always loved to play with the animals of the forest and make friends with them.

One day, while Milo was playing beside a quiet river, a young lion named Leo came near. Leo was a big, strong lion, and the other animals always treated him with respect. But Milo was not afraid of Leo. Instead, he became Leo's friend.

Milo and Leo had a wonderful time together. Milo told Leo how he played with the other creatures of the forest, and Leo taught him how to climb the trees to pick oranges and tomatoes. The two of them treated each other as good friends.

One day, Milo did not have a good day. He felt as if the world was being unkind, and that made him feel bad. He went to Leo and said he could never do anything at all. Leo smiled at Milo and told him that every child feels that way sometimes. He told Milo that Milo was a good child, and that he could begin again and do the things he wished to do.

Milo learned that he could always try. He began to work and to help others. Whenever he felt tired, Leo reminded him that he was always there.

This story teaches us that friendship can help us make our world a kinder place.

The End.
