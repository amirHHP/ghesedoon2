Once upon a time, in a beautiful, quiet village beside a bright, full river, there lived four children named Arman, Artin, Melika, and Leila. These four friends were always looking for adventure, and they loved to discover new things. One warm, sunny summer day, while they were playing beside the river, their eyes fell on a small magic boat resting quietly on the shore.

The boat, with beautiful, shining colors like a rainbow, drew them close. The boat looked very old and, at the same time, magical. Arman, who was always full of courage and adventure, said to his friends, “Let’s go on a magic journey with this boat and see what adventures are waiting for us!”

All the friends agreed with excitement. They climbed carefully and eagerly into the boat. As soon as they were all seated, the boat began to move slowly, as if it already knew where to go. The river water flowed gently under the boat, and the magic boat set off toward unknown adventures.

Along the way, they enjoyed the beautiful views all around. Colorful flowers, tall trees, and sweet-singing birds filled every place. Suddenly the boat turned slowly and entered a hidden water road the children had never seen before.

This water road brought them to a magic forest. In this forest, everything was shiny and bright. The trees were full of silver leaves and golden flowers, and strange birds with rainbow wings flew all around. The children looked everywhere with eyes full of surprise and wonder.

Melika, who always loved flowers, went to one of the golden flowers and picked it. At once the flower began to speak and said, “Hello! I am a magic flower. If you wish, I can take you anywhere you want.”

Artin said with excitement, “We want to go to the Island of Sweets!” The golden flower smiled and, with a magic spin, guided the boat toward the Island of Sweets.

The boat passed quickly through the magic forest and suddenly reached an island full of colorful sweets. This island was full of chocolate houses, rivers of milk, and hills of cake. The children ran happily toward the sweets and began to eat all kinds of candies and chocolates.

Leila, who always noticed the little details, suddenly saw a big chocolate tree with a small door. She called her friends and said, “Let’s see what is hidden behind this door!”

When they opened the little door, they discovered a new world full of magic, fun games. This magic world was full of playful traps, talking toys, and lovable animals. The children played for hours in this wonderful world and enjoyed every moment.

When the sun was setting, the magic boat gently brought the children back to their village. They went home with hearts full of happy memories and lips full of laughter and joy. Arman, Artin, Melika, and Leila all understood that with friendship and courage they could go anywhere and try any adventure.

From that day on, the children were always looking for new adventures together, and the magic boat was always ready to take them on magic journeys.

The End.
