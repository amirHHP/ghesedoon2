In a faraway forest there lived a fox named Rudy. He was a smart, clever fox who was always looking for adventure and fun. Rudy always had tricks for playing with his friends, but unlike other foxes, he never wanted anyone to get hurt.

One day, Rudy went to visit his friends in the forest. All the animals were happy and playing together. A little monkey boy named Mickey said to Rudy, “Dear Rudy, you always make us glad with your tricks. Do you have a trick today too?”

Rudy smiled and said to Mickey, “Yes. Today I have a new trick. I can fool everyone!”

All the animals said with excitement, “How fun! Show us!”

Rudy told them, “I have a pretty magic puppet that can talk!”

The animals thought to themselves, “Really? How wonderful!”

Rudy quickly made a puppet with a glove and a stick. When he was done, he said, “Now look at the magic puppet!”

He held the puppet and began to talk in a very funny voice. All the animals were amazed and thought Rudy had truly made a magic puppet.

Mickey the monkey laughed and said, “Rudy, you really are a professional at tricks!”

Rudy smiled and moved the puppet for his friends. Everyone stared at it in surprise and was happy to have a new game.

After a while, Rudy laughed and said, “Kids, this puppet isn’t real! I made it myself with a glove and a stick!”

All the animals laughed and said, “Dear Rudy, you always make us glad!”

From that day on, everyone loved to play with Rudy, and he always cheered them up with new tricks. But no one ever forgot the magic puppet, and they always remembered that Rudy had creative ways to play.

The End.
