Once upon a time, in a faraway forest, there lived a special bird called the Rainbow Bird. This bird was very beautiful, and its feathers were many colors: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and purple. Everyone who saw it was amazed by the beauty of those feathers.

The Rainbow Bird was very happy and always flew through the sky with a smile. It loved to play with the other birds and animals of the forest, and it amazed everyone with the beauty of its feathers.

But one day, something sad happened. One of the rainbow feathers, the yellow one, was lost. The Rainbow Bird flew about in worry and searched for the missing feather. It looked all through the forest, but it could not find the yellow feather.

After a while, the Rainbow Bird came back and told its friends, "My yellow feather is lost. I do not know how to find it." The Rainbow Bird's friends felt sad, and they helped search for the missing feather.

After more searching, the Rainbow Bird suddenly saw a trail of rainbow on the ground. It flew quickly toward that trail, and when it came close, it saw that its yellow feather was resting on the earth.

The Rainbow Bird picked up the yellow feather with joy and flew back up into the sky. It showed all its friends that the missing feather had been found, and everyone was happy together.

From then on, the Rainbow Bird always knew to check all its feathers carefully so that none would be lost. It always remembered the yellow feather, and it reminded everyone to love their friends and family and to help one another when help is needed.

This story reminds us that in friendship and togetherness we should always help one another, and we should always take care of each other.

The End.
