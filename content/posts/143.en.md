Once upon a time, in a little village beside a green forest, there lived a little girl named Leila. Leila loved nature, and she always wanted to get to know plants and animals. From her mother and father she had learned many things about healing plants and the gifts of different greenery.

One beautiful day, Leila decided to go looking for healing plants in the forest. She left the house with a little bag and walked toward the trees. Along the way she recognized different plants she had heard about in her parents' stories. She found plants with sweet-smelling leaves, plants with colorful flowers, and even plants that were helpful for feeling better.

Deep in the forest, Leila came upon a special healing plant. This plant had big blue leaves and was surrounded by beautiful purple flowers. Leila looked at the plant more carefully. From her mother and father she had learned that this plant was very helpful for a cold and for making the body strong.

Leila walked around the plant with care and gathered more news about it. She knew well how this plant could bring the best help to others. She collected the beautiful purple flowers along with the blue leaves in her bag and went home feeling happy.

From then on, Leila told everyone how she had found this special healing plant in the forest, and they all enjoyed her interesting, wonderful story.

Leila's story shows us that sometimes, close to home, we can discover very precious things we never thought about before.

The End.
