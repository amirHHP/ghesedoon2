On a starry night, a little boy named Arad sat in the yard of their house with his mother. Arad looked at the starry sky and was amazed by all those bright, beautiful stars. He said to his mother with curiosity, “Mama, why do the stars move in the sky?”

Arad’s mother answered with a kind smile, “The stars look as if they are moving, but really it is the Earth that is turning. Let’s hear a story about how the stars move across the sky.”

Arad perked up his ears with excitement to hear the story. His mother began, “A very long time ago, in a big galaxy full of stars, there lived a little star named Nooran. Nooran was very bright and shiny, and she always wanted to travel to different places in the sky.”

One night, Nooran decided to join her friends, the other stars, and go on a wonderful journey together. They flew through the sky and visited different parts of the galaxy. Nooran was surprised to see that every star had its own place and moved slowly along a special path.

One of the stars, named Sparkle, said to Nooran, “We stars are always moving. Some of us circle the galaxy on special paths, and others travel in big groups called star clusters.”

Nooran asked, “Why do we move?”

Sparkle answered, “We move because of gravity and the different forces in the galaxy. All of us are pulled by these forces, and we move along special paths. Also, the Earth is spinning, and that is why it looks as if we are moving across the sky.”

Nooran looked at the sky in wonder and understood that the movement of the stars is part of the beauty and wonder of the galaxy. She said to her friends, “Our movement as stars makes the sky beautiful and different every night. That is so wonderful!”

Arad smiled when he heard this story and looked at the sky. Now he understood why the stars move in the sky. Arad said to his mother, “Mama, I want to be like Nooran too, and discover new things.”

Arad’s mother answered kindly, “You can, dear Arad. Every night when you look at the sky, you can learn new things and discover the wonders of the galaxy.”

The nights passed, and every night Arad looked at the starry sky and thought with wonder about the movement of the stars. Each time he looked at the stars, he remembered the story of Nooran and her friends and enjoyed the beauty of the sky.

One night, Arad said to his mother, “Mama, I want to be a scientist when I grow up and learn more about stars and galaxies.”

Arad’s mother looked at him with pride and said, “Dear Arad, you can be anything you want. Always remember that with curiosity and hard work, you can reach your wishes.”

And so, every night Arad looked at the starry sky with eagerness and enjoyed how the stars moved. He had learned that stars are not only beautiful, but they also hold many stories that can teach us a great deal.

Every day Arad grew more interested in studying and learning, and he eagerly wanted to discover the secrets of the galaxy. He promised himself that one day he would become a great scientist and help others learn more about the beauties and wonders of the sky.

And that is how the story of Arad and the movement of the stars came to an end, but his love for the sky and the galaxy never ended. Every day Arad wanted even more to learn and discover new things, and he always looked at the sky with eyes full of wonder.

The End.
