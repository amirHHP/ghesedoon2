In a small, beautiful town, life was full of joy, color, and magic. In this town lived a little girl named Leila who always loved to make magic paintings with her colored pencils. Her pencils added any color she wished to the world around her, and everything she colored with them grew even happier and more colorful.

Early one morning, Leila sat in her room with her colored pencils and painted flowers, birds, and magic animals. With her yellow pencil she painted a sun in the sky, and with her green pencil she drew trees and lush grass. With her blue pencil she pictured a clear, flowing river, and with her red pencil she painted beautiful magic flowers in many colors.

One day, Leila decided to travel to a new world with her magic colored pencils. With her orange pencil she made a tree of magic fruit inside an apricot garden, and with her purple pencil she painted a palace with tall, glorious towers. Then, with her gray pencil, she pictured a calm lake with crystal-blue water and magic fish swimming inside.

With every picture she made, Leila created a new world of color and magic, and everyone who saw these pictures filled with wonder and joy. She told everyone that with magic colored pencils you can color anything you love and bring a colorful, magical world to life.

Leila’s story reminds children that with their colored pencils they can turn any color they love into a magic world, and that with painting they can make life beautiful and full of joy — just like Leila in this story.

The End.
