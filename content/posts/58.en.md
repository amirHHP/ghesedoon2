Once upon a time, in a small, beautiful village, there lived two brothers named Ali and Mohammad. Ali was a happy, lively child who was always looking for adventure and joy. Mohammad was older than Ali, and he loved to read stories and escape into a make-believe world.

Every night, their mother told them stories. These stories were always full of magic and adventure, and they carried the two brothers into a new and different world.

One night, their mother held a wonderful book in her hands. It was a book with a magic world inside, and stories they had never heard. Ali and Mohammad took the book with wonder and asked their mother to read one of its stories to them.

Their mother smiled and said, “Come, tonight I will read you one of the nighttime tales from this book.” She chose a story called “Adventures of Courage and Friendship.” It was about two close friends who went on adventures together and solved problems together.

Their mother told the story in a calm, sweet voice. She explained how the two friends helped each other and, with courage and faith in friendship, left every trouble behind. Ali and Mohammad listened carefully, and both of them took courage and friendship as a guide in their own lives.

When the story was over, Ali and Mohammad thanked their mother and told her how much they loved the nighttime tales. Their mother smiled and said, “Nighttime tales can always open a gate into your world and take you on adventures you will never forget.”

From that day on, Ali and Mohammad waited every night with eagerness for their mother to read them a wonderful new story. Holding hands, the two of them slipped into a make-believe world full of adventure and friendship, and they never grew tired of listening to nighttime tales.

The End.
