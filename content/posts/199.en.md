Once upon a time, in a little village called Cheer, there lived a little boy named Mehran. Mehran loved to ride his bicycle around the village streets, and every day he went on adventures with his friends.

One day, Mehran and his friends — Niloufar, Kian, and Arman — decided to take a journey to the forest on their bicycles. With excitement and joy they got their bicycles ready and set off toward the forest.

Mehran, a brave, energetic little boy, was going to the forest for the first time. He said to his friends with eagerness, “Playing in the forest will be so interesting! I can’t wait to see the animals and the trees of the forest.”

Niloufar, a gentle, kind little girl, always helped her friends, and today too she said to them with a good feeling, “Let’s make good memories together and enjoy a happy time with one another.”

Kian, a creative, clever little boy, always loved to see new things, and today he was very happy to see the forest and its animals. He said to everyone, “With our bicycles we can go everywhere and see everything.”

Arman, a sweet-voiced, lovable little boy, always smiled at his friends, and today too he smiled at everyone and said, “Together we can do anything, and it will always be fun with one another.”

The friends rode their bicycles toward the forest, and each of them enjoyed the moments of play in nature. They always helped one another with care, and they enjoyed seeing the different plants and animals in the forest.

At the height of the journey they reached a beautiful spring whose water was very cool and sweet. The friends drank from the spring with pleasure and told one another what a good memory this journey had been. They understood that playing together and helping one another is the best way to joy and happiness.

And that is how, with their bicycles, Mehran, Niloufar, Kian, and Arman always remembered to play together and make a happy time for one another, wherever they are and whenever they want.

The End.
