In the village of birds, where all the birds lived together and woke up early every morning with their songs, there lived a special bird named Pip. Pip was a very special bird, the sound of his song captivated everyone.

Every morning, Pip sat on a high branch of a tree in the middle of the bird village. As his eyes opened, he began to sing. Pip's beautiful voice, like a magical poem, stirred and attracted everyone. Small and big birds, from all corners of the village, gathered to hear the sound of the pipe.

One day, a boy named Leo, who had always heard Pip's beautiful voice in the distance, decided to come and see Pip. He sat by the tree where Pip was sitting, secretly like a secret guest and listened to Pip's song.

Pip heard Leo's voice, but saw no one. He was happy to have a new friend and sang to him. Pip's singing was so beautiful that Leo couldn't even hold back his tears. From that day on, every morning, Leo came to listen to the pipe and hear its beautiful song.

Pip and Leo became good friends and always spent time together. Leo sometimes brings Pip small gifts, such as colorful hooks or wild flowers. These gifts were a sign of Leo and Pip's friendship.

From then on, the bird village was full of joy and happiness every day with Pip's beautiful songs, and everyone followed him to enjoy his singing.

The End.
