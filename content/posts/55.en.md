Once upon a time, in a faraway forest, there lived a special bird named the Happiness Bird. This bird had yellow wings and feathers that shone like crystal, and he was always smiling and glad. He had a special gift: he could bring happiness to others.

The Happiness Bird hopped and fluttered from tree to tree. Wherever he went, he brought cheer all around him. He looked for new friends and played with the children of the forest. With a happy, beautiful song, the Happiness Bird made everyone laugh and filled their hearts with happiness.

One day, the Happiness Bird met a little child named Arian. Arian was a shy little boy who felt tired of life in the forest. He was often alone, and he did not talk with the other children. But when the Happiness Bird sang and flew through the air with his wings, Arian suddenly felt bright and happy.

Arian came near the Happiness Bird, a little shy, and said, “How can you always be so happy?”

The Happiness Bird said with a warm smile, “I know that happiness lives inside me, and I share this happiness with others. Anyone who is near me can feel joy and happiness.”

Arian was so glad to hear those words. From then on he always looked for the Happiness Bird and played with him. Every day, the Happiness Bird taught Arian and his friends how to live with happiness and how to share it. They played together, they laughed together, and they always had good memories to keep.

The Happiness Bird was glad with all his heart that he could give happiness to others. He taught everyone that happiness lives inside us, and we must never let it go.

After that, the forest was always full of laughter and happiness, and everyone looked at the Happiness Bird as a magic bird who brought happiness to all.

The End.
