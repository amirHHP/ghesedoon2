In a small, faraway village, the life of the mosquitoes was full of happiness and calm. Every day, the little mosquitoes with their clear, shining wings flew around the flowers and trees and enjoyed the sweets of the flowers and the tasty fruits.

One of these mosquitoes was named Kitty. Kitty was a little, cheerful mosquito who was always looking for adventure and discovering new things. She always hopped with her friends in the village and traveled up to the top of the sky to see the beauties of the village well.

One day, Kitty decided to go on a big journey. She told her friends very soon, and everyone congratulated her and wished her well. With long, lively wing-beats, Kitty left her village and flew toward the world outside.

On her journey, Kitty made friends with big, beautiful birds who flew around the trees and streams. She also met the big, pleasant bears who slept in the shade under the trees, and she became their friend. All the animals welcomed Kitty and made her happy on her journey.

During the journey, Kitty reached a flowing, beautiful river. She skated on her feathers and passed over the flowing waters of the river with pleasure. Around the river, swallows flew among the trees, looking for the sun under the blue sky, and they had a calm, happy life.

Kitty’s journey came to its end, and she returned home with energy and the beautiful memories of this trip and told her friends. She always thought of the memories of this journey with a smile, and the whole village welcomed her beautiful stories.

This story reminds children that with the courage to discover the world around them, they can have beautiful, enjoyable experiences—just like Kitty, the little mosquito in this story.

The End.
